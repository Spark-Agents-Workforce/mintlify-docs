The program has three parts. Part one shows you how to use it. Part two banks twelve wins on your existing stack in week one. Parts three to eight teach the machines behind your wins.

1. **[Use this program](/growth-program-v2/start/how-this-program-works)** — the guide, the wins ledger, and the five-question triage. Five minutes.
2. **[The week-one sprint](/growth-program-v2/sprint/index)** — twelve wins. Nine ship a change. Each ends with one number in your ledger.
3. **[Read your numbers](/growth-program-v2/numbers/why-your-wins-worked)** — why your wins worked, and your ranked leak list in dollars.
4. **[Plug the leaks](/growth-program-v2/billing/dunning)** — failed payments, the first sixty days, cancellations and saves, winback.
5. **[Email and SMS](/growth-program-v2/messaging/klaviyo-architecture)** — the messaging engine your wins already run through.
6. **[Get more subscribers](/growth-program-v2/offer/the-discount-question)** — the offer, the storefront, traffic, big sale events.
7. **[Memberships and loyalty](/growth-program-v2/membership/memberships)** — what keeps subscribers beyond the discount.
8. **[Run it like an operator](/growth-program-v2/run/the-operating-rhythm)** — the rhythm, honest testing, when to get help.

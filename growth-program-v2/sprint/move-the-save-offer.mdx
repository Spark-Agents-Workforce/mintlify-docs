Open your cancel flow and count the screens between the cancel click and your save offer. If the reason survey comes first, nobody chose that — it shipped that way, and nobody has audited it since. The order turns out to be the lever: across 800k cancel sessions, brands showing an offer *before* the reason step saved 26.6% of cancellations vs 13.8% without (seller). {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#008 — canon row 30 */} Per hundred subscribers walking into the flow, that is roughly thirteen more still standing — for a screen reorder. {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#008 — derived: 26.6 − 13.8 ≈ 12.8 per 100 entrants */} No new offer, no deeper discount: the brands saving roughly double the share are the ones whose offer fires first.

A subscriber in a cancel flow is walking to the door, and every screen between their click and your offer adds momentum. An offer on the first screen catches them at the coat rack, still deciding. An offer after a three-question survey is chasing them across the parking lot. Now the honest label: this is Skio's own network, read by Skio, which sells the toggle — correlational, not a randomized test, and brands that configure offer screens likely invest more in retention overall. {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#008 (caveat) */} It is also the only step-order evidence at this scale, and acting on it is a reorder of screens you already own.

{/* shot:01 keystone-stat "Offer before the reason survey: 26.6% of cancellations saved. Without one: 13.8%." */}

Three competing vendors ship this exact screen today: Recharge a Deflection Page, Skio a Splash Screen (current builder, February 2026), Stay AI a Pre-Survey Screen (January 2026). {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#035, save-cancellation-flow#036, save-cancellation-flow#037 */} Your platform already built the button.

**Time:** 10–30 minutes · **You'll touch:** your subscription platform's cancel-flow editor · **Result shows:** days

<Warning>
**Keep the cancel button visible.** Under California's ARL, any save offer in the cancel flow must sit beside a simultaneously visible cancel button (effective July 1, 2025). {/* cid:meta-category-playbook#015, convert-pdp#023 — canon row 36 */} If you add or move an offer screen, make sure that a clear cancel path shows on that same screen.
</Warning>

    Cancellation Prevention flows apply to the Shopify Checkout integrations — for most Shopify stores, that is you. {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#035 */} Recharge is also mid-rollout of a new portal nav, so menus can drift.

    <Steps>

      <Step title="Place the offer first">
        Drag the **Deflection Page** node onto the canvas, directly before the cancellation survey node. {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#035 */}
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the offer">
        Click the node. Select the offer that this page shows — for example, a delay, a pause, a discount, a gift, or a swap.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Keep the cancel path">
        Make sure that the page keeps a visible option to continue the cancellation.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Activate and test">
        Click **Save**, then **Activate**. Then do a test: start a cancellation in your own customer portal.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

      </Step>
    </Steps>

    If the menus moved, search Skio help for **Splash Screen** or **Cancel compliance settings**.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the surveys">
        In the merchant portal, go to **Optimization > Cancellation Surveys**. Stay allows one active survey, and Active surveys lock structural edits. {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#037 */}
      </Step>
      <Step title="Duplicate before you edit">
        Duplicate your live survey. Edit the draft copy.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the Pre-Survey Screen">

      </Step>
      <Step title="Configure the accept path">
        If no **Offer Accepted** screen exists, configure that screen first. The Pre-Survey Screen requires it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the starter node">

      </Step>
      <Step title="Activate">
        Activate the survey. Write the ship date in your ledger.
      </Step>

<Note>
Worried about the reason survey? It still runs — after the offer instead of before it. On Recharge, subscribers who accept bypass the survey. Decliners still answer it. You lose reasons only from the people who stayed. And the only controlled test found favors optimized reason-agnostic offers (~2x save) over reason-matched routing — reason data diagnoses; it doesn't have to gate (seller). {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#029 — canon row 34 */}
</Note>

## You'll see

| Platform | Read the save rate at |
| --- | --- |
| Recharge | Churn tools > Cancellation prevention > your flow > **⋮ More actions > Reports**, Performance tab |
| Skio | **Analytics > Cancel Flow Dashboard**, the "Cancel flow performance" block — updates every 4 hours {/* cid:numbers-kpi-dashboard#003 */} |
| Stay AI | **Analytics > Cancel Survey**, "Save Rate Through Cancel Survey" |

Record the save rate and the entrant count for the 30 days before your change. The entrant count lives on that same screen — Recharge calls it **Experiences delivered**, and Skio and Stay count cancel-flow sessions. After the change, read the same screen. Compare only numbers from the same definition.

<Note>
Platform save rates count survey abandoners as saved — treat every dashboard save rate as a deflection metric, never a retention outcome. {/* cid:save-churn-diagnostics#019, numbers-kpi-dashboard#015, numbers-kpi-dashboard#016 — canon row 26 */} Roughly a quarter of cancel-flow entrants deflect in-session but only ~17% are still saved at 30 days — always quote both numbers (seller). {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#005 — canon row 27 */} Your before/after survives that inflation because the definition holds constant. On Stay AI it does not hold across the 2026 survey switch — never compare a new-survey save rate to a RetentionEngine-era one. {/* cid:numbers-kpi-dashboard#016 */}
</Note>

A 5-point save-rate move reads at about 905 entrants on each side of the change, on a 15% base. {/* cid:run-operating-cadence#010 */} If 300 subscribers enter your cancel flow each month, that is roughly three months per side. Do not call the result at week one — the dashboard moves daily, the verdict does not.

For scale: Skio's named case, Cornbread Hemp, went from a 9.5% save rate to 25.6% — after adding a splash screen and reworking its offers in the same push, so read it as a vendor-published ceiling, not a promise. {/* cid:save-cancellation-flow#009 */}

**Checkpoint:** the save rate rises on the same definition, and the offer screen shows a visible cancel path.

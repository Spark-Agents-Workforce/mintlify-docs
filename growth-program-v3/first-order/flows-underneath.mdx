- The scoreboard: activation % within 30 days; flow benchmarks under it (50-65% open, 5-12% click, 5-15% review collection, 8-20% repeat purchase).
- The welcome flow branches first-time vs repeat buyer (repeat gets loyalty acknowledged, brand education skipped).
- Flow hygiene: buyers auto-exit prospect flows; 16-hour minimum gap between automated sends.
- Personalization: real account context only (name, milestone, what they bought); broken personalization is worse than none.
- Coupons: unique per-subscriber codes, never static codes that leak.
- The data handoff: what they browsed and which claim converted them moves from acquisition into the ritual emails.
- The 23-hour incomplete-order window: the only churn that happens before the relationship starts.

<Info>
**Kit, not a box:** unboxing IS the ritual equipment arriving. **Frontier:** zero corpus cards on any physical object at a home — the deepest gap in the deck; written from CPG first principles.
</Info>

- The box experience inventory (JD 8/29): shipping box, tape, inner box, packing contents, the first-unboxing moment, what they're told to **do** during first unboxing, where to store it.
- The startup guide / recipe / tutorial that drives the habit.
- The kit-ritual bridge: if the offer promised a starter kit (ramen bowl + spoon + chopsticks), delivery day is where the ritual activates.
- Operational: tag kit/gift orders so fulfillment and flows know first shipment from rebill.
- Unboxing as expectation-setting: say at unboxing what day 30/60/90 feel like (the survival-kit move).
- Insert that shows the ritual; QR to the 60-second how-to ([PROOF] slots).
- Delivery-timed sends run off Fulfilled Order, never the charge date.
- Usage tips at delivery +2-3 days; review ask at delivery +7-10 days.
- The genuinely handwritten card: two-three sentences, one personal detail.
- Delivery distance is a top-5 churn driver (vendor model).
- Out-of-stock: reschedule, never cancel — gentler than card-failure handling.
- Delivery-failure make-good: extend the renewal date so they never pay for the gap.

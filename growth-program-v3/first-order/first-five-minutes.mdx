<Info>
**Frontier:** the thank-you page as activation surface — zero corpus cards; we write it first: what arrives when, the one thing to do before the box lands, the reply channel.
</Info>

- The account portal's first visit: what boots up when they land there.
- The subscription welcome email, separate from Shopify's receipt.
- One primary next step per email, never a feature list.
- Dates in plain text: what ships when, when the first renewal charges.
- The P.S. that invites a reply (day-zero complaint channel, skips the public bad review).
- Every email carries a fallback plain URL for when the button fails.
- The receipt as three plays: next-purchase coupon with an expiry, statement descriptor + card last-four in plain text (chargeback insurance), proactive card-update prompt keyed to life events.
- Capture the second payment method **now**, framed never-miss-a-box — backup card +31% to +51% LTV (vendor math); [The crack](/growth-program-v3/the-crack) teaches recovery, this chapter only captures.
- The promise of next contact: they always know when they'll hear from you; first follow-up inside 24-48 hours.

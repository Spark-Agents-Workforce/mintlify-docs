- The template: every customer who does X stays longer than those who don't.
- Find your X from your own stayers: six-step derivation (churned data, 3+ month stayers, top 20% by spend, what they share, week-one difference, test five candidates).
- Re-derive the X every 6-12 months, it drifts.
- Poor product adoption is unsubscribe reason number one (corpus): the named churn cause, not a soft metric.
- Routine-builder: usage-education emails + short-dated incentive (10% coupon, 14-day expiry — the deadline is the point).
- Order-one loyalty rewards cut first-order churn 30-40% (vendor); tier unlock belongs at order **two** (Skio 3-order frame: one mechanic per order, never a price discount).
- The format ladder: custom > generic, personal > group, live > recorded, carrots > sticks, some > none. A segment-cut email is Custom; a founder voice note is nearly Live.
- Ride the activation high: the ascension/add-on ask lands **now**, not after "settled" (just-bought customers are the least likely to churn).
- The public review at delivery +7-10 days is a lock-in device (public endorsement makes leaving psychologically harder).
- Pre-frame the exit conversation: told during onboarding, reads as process when [Jumped out](/growth-program-v3/jumped-out) needs it.

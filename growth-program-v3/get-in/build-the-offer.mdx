## The diagnostic opener

- Pull three numbers from your own store before any teaching: your current first-order discount, your first-renewal rate, your subscription take rate.
- The financial model: 5% vs 10% vs 15% vs 20% vs 25% and deeper — cohort math per band, kit/shaker COGS in the first shipment.
- Channel coherence: your DTC subscription must be the best offer anywhere you sell, Amazon included, delta shown.

## What they pay

- The depth curve: 21M subscriptions — 65 of 100 renew at no discount, 53 at 90%+ off (vendor).
- Why the second bill breaks: they memorize the joining price, so the rebill reads as a price hike.
- The default: under 25% off.
- "10% subscribe-and-save is safe on the curve and converts nobody, the offer needs a reason beyond the discount."
- The discount-placement variants: second-order discount, step-down pricing.
- \$0.99 beats free: paying anything makes them a buyer.
- Deep discounts are survivable only with an order-two plan, plus the four-move rescue kit.
- Bundles for bigger savings; 30/60/90-day supplies and prepaid.

## What if they hate it

- **The trial, the flagship:** your existing guarantee renamed — same refund liability, lighter yes; discounts commitment instead of price.
- The guarantee itself.

<Warning>
The legal spine: the renewal price is disclosed before the trial starts, and cancel is as easy as start.
</Warning>

- The escape valve shown **before** the yes: pause, skip, one-click cancel — said out loud at signup.

## What shows up in the box

- The free gift.
- The starter kit as ritual equipment: the bowl, the spoon, the shaker — the object the habit forms around.
- The guide/PDF that starts the routine.

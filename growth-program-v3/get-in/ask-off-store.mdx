## Pop-ups

- Earn the interruption with the trial, not a naked discount for an email address.
- One field, button says what happens next.

## Browse abandonment

- Lead with the program angle, not the product they declined.
- The trial, the one-click exit, the weekly price.

## Come-back-to-store emails

- The lapsed one-time buyer gets the program pitch.
- The argument is the rhythm they abandoned: this product runs out — the program exists so it doesn't.

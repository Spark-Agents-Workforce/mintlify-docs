## The dedicated page

- /subscribe: own URL, written about the program not any SKU, own proof from subscribers.
- Subscriber benefits live here: faster shipping, first access to new flavors and restocks.
- The ad destination for subscription-intent traffic; the link every outbound message points at.
- If the subscription only exists as a widget, it's a payment option; this page makes it a product.

## The defaults

- PDP defaults to subscription: pre-selected, real price gap shown, cadence options.
- The take-rate target and its warning: pushed too hard it fills cycle two with quitters — taught as a test.
- Collections default: the offer at card level, met before the click.

<Warning>
Stated law: the cadence they pick at signup is Get in writing checks [First weeks](/growth-program-v3/first-weeks) must cash.
</Warning>

## The always-on surfaces

- The hero carries the program.
- The top bar carries the trial line or the one-click-cancel promise — never a naked percentage.
- Easy in, one-click out, advertised out loud.

## The last-chance surfaces

- The cart: every one-time item carries the switch offer, delta in dollars.
- Checkout: the switch once more, one line, nothing new introduced.
- Post-purchase: the cheapest yes on the store — one-click upgrade, kept brief.

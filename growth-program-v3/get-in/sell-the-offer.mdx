## The product page

- Subscription beside one-time, savings delta in dollars.
- Bigger supply first, monthly as the step-down.
- Weekly price display, four-week billing — both reconciled on the same surface.
- .99 endings, luxury round-number exception.
- The trial line placed where the benefit lands.
- The kit/gift shown as part of what you get.

## Landing pages

- One page per claim you run traffic against, never one generic.
- Problem, agitate, solve; the trial as the close.
- The page inherits the offer exactly — no page-only variants.

## The bundle builder

- The shopper assembles the bigger order; the anchor becomes their idea.
- Every bundle path ends at the same subscription ask.

## The writing laws

Governs every surface in this chapter and beyond:

- Every section justifies itself in one sentence or gets cut.
- The five questions in order: what is this, why care, how it works, who else, what next.
- The three tests: headlines alone, no images, outside-the-category reader.
- Buttons say what happens next; benefits before features; concrete numbers; one program name sitewide.
- Proof from subscribers specifically, collected on a standing system.

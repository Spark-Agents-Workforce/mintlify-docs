- The clock starts at the click; the bucket ends on the counter.
- Activation point defined: the first use of the consumable (scoop, pour, apply).
- Scoreboard: % of new subscribers hitting first use within 30 days.
- Month-one churn spike is the tree being shaken, not failure (10-15% month one, 7% month two, 3% month three — vendor trajectory).
- Complaint stakes: a new subscriber with an active complaint is 3-4x more likely to churn; everything wrong gets answered before day 30.

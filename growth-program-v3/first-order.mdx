Grown-ups call it activation. On the track it is the stretch between two clicks — the one that started the subscription and the first scoop, pour, or application that makes it real. The window runs from order placed, through the wait, to the box landing, and it ends at one precise moment: the product has a physical place and a ritual in the customer's day. On the counter, not the back of the pantry.

[Get in](/growth-program-v3/get-in) ended at the click. [First weeks](/growth-program-v3/first-weeks) starts at order two. This bucket is everything in between.

## The figure

Three numbers define the window. The shake-the-tree spike: churn can rise up to 50% in month one as half-decided subscribers surface, then drop dramatically by months two and three — the playbook's worked trajectory runs 10% to 15%, then 7%, then 3%. The diagnosis: high month-one-to-two churn maps to exactly one cause — no defined activation point and weak onboarding. The clock: a low-tenure customer with an active complaint is 3-4x more likely to churn, so everything that goes wrong in this window gets answered before day 30.

And one number grades the whole bucket: **the percentage of new subscribers who hit the activation point within 30 days.**

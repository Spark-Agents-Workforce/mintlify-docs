## Dark stage (os-dark-stage)

The authored ceremony surface (/threshold). Warm charcoal `--nh2-dark` #211C11 at band or page scale — an authored surface, never a theme toggle; `appearance.strict` stays light. On-dark pair: headings `--nh2-dark-heading` #FFF8E8, body `--nh2-dark-body` #D9D2C3. Ledger rules use the dark-side hairline token `--nh2-dark-hairline` rgba(255,248,232,0.12) — token-level, never inline. Chip stays the one caps surface; #000 hard shadows on charcoal read as a quiet deboss — that is the decided dark-stage artifact look, never "fixed" with a glow. Yellow scarcity is stage law: on a full dark page, one chip and one pill, nothing else saturated.

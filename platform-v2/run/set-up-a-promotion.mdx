Promotions are offers shown in the customer portal to subscribers you choose: a banner with a one-tap action.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    - Open **Settings > Promotions** and create a promotion. The editor runs top to bottom: **Details** (name it), **Creative** (message and look), **Call to action** (what the button does), **Placement** (where it shows; one promotion can hold several).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the banner says one thing and the button does it.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/32-promotion-editor.png" alt="The promotion editor: details, creative, call to action, placement, and targeting, top to bottom." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aim it">
    - **Targeting** picks the audience: conditions combined with **ALL of** / **ANY of**, plus a live estimate of matching subscribers. The condition builder works like the one in [bulk operations](/platform-v2/run/change-many-at-once).
    - **Schedule**, in the right sidebar, bounds a seasonal promotion; leave it open-ended if standing.
    - Press **Activate** when ready; until then it's a draft no subscriber sees.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the estimate matches who you meant and the badge reads **Active**.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it, and retire it">
    - **Performance** tracks impressions, clicks, and conversions, plus revenue for the **Add product** and **Apply discount** calls to action (others show revenue as not tracked), with **Export stats** and **Export activity** CSVs.
    - Pause a promotion to stop it showing; archive it when done. Stats stay.
    - A temporary discount that can't be taken back off a subscription surfaces as a banner on the promotion's page and as the **Promo revert abandoned** alert on **Settings > Operational health** ([When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens)).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the funnel moves and nothing asks for attention.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

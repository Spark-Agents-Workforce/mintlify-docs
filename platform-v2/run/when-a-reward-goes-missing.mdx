---
title: "When a reward goes missing"
description: "Revert, then redeem: the two clicks that put a removed free gift back."
---

import { PlatformTag } from "/snippets/PlatformTag.jsx";

<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

Loyalty rewards ([Set up rewards](/platform-v2/run/set-up-rewards)) come in two kinds: free gifts the subscriber redeems, and discounts that apply to the next order by themselves. Everything you need is on the subscription's page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the reward's state first">
    - Open the subscription and find the **Reward history** card: every reward this customer earned, with a status.

    | Status | Meaning |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Available** | Earned, not yet used. |
    | **Redeemed** | On the upcoming order. |
    | **Processed** | Billed onto an order, with a **View order** link. |

    - The **Customer** card shows the running count of recurring orders toward rewards.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the status names your case; the steps below match.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/23-reward-history.png" alt="Reward history: discounts and free gifts with Redeemed and Processed statuses, Revert on a redeemed gift." width="640" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put a removed free gift back">
    - The scenario: the gift line is gone from **Items**, but **Reward history** still reads **Redeemed**.
    - Click **Revert** on the gift's row and confirm; the reward returns to **Available**.
    - In **Available rewards**, above the history, click **Redeem** and confirm with **Add gift**. The line returns to **Items** at zero, marked **Free Gift**.

    <Warning>
    **Don't re-add the gift with Add product.** That creates a paid line: the subscriber gets billed for their own reward.
    </Warning>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the gift is back at zero, history row **Redeemed** again.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/24-available-rewards.png" alt="Available rewards: an earned free gift one Redeem away from the subscription." width="640" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A gift that vanished after an order isn't missing">
    - A gift belongs to one order: once billed, the line leaves the subscription by itself and the history row reads **Processed**, with a **View order** link. Nothing to fix.
    - Merging subscriptions strands gift lines on the retired subscription; an unshipped gift there still reads **Redeemed**, and **Revert** works from the surviving subscription. **Unable to revert the reward gift** means the retired contract is no longer editable in Shopify: raise it with whoever runs your code.

    <Note>
    **Not every free gift is a reward.** A save-offer gift from your cancellation flow looks identical but has no Reward history row, so Revert and Redeem never show for it.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The discount that never appeared">
    - Amount-off and percentage-off rewards apply themselves and show on the **Payment summary** card as **Reward Discount**, good for one order.
    - A customer with several subscriptions gets the discount on whichever bills next: check the siblings first.
    - Remove buttons on the **Payment summary** target promo codes, not reward discounts.
    - A discount that couldn't land yet applies as renewals advance; one that gave up shows on **Settings > Operational health** ([When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens)).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** one subscription shows **Reward Discount**, or Operational health explains why not.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="When Redeem is refused">
    - "Reward not found" usually means it was redeemed already: refresh and re-read the history.
    - "Unable to add reward gift" usually means the gift product or its variant no longer exists; the history row falls back to "Free gift" with no price. Restore the product in Shopify and redeem again.
    - If the product is gone for good, pick a new gift for the tier under **Settings > Rewards** and make this subscriber whole.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the gift redeems clean, or the refusal is identified.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

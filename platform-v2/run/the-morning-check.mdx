<span className="os-chip">5 min</span>

Check that the machine from [What runs without you](/platform-v2/run/what-runs-without-you) ran overnight: two screens, plus a weekly look at Analytics.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    - Open the OpenSubs admin; it lands on Home.
    - **Needs attention** appears only when something needs you: subscriptions with payment trouble and operations alerts awaiting review. **Review subscriptions** opens the list filtered to **Billing issues**; **Review operations** opens **Settings > Operational health** (step 2).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/02-home-needs-attention.png" alt="Home: the Needs attention banner counting an open operations alert." width="640" />
    </Frame>

    - Glance at **Store health** (revenue, new and cancelled subscriptions over 7 days, current active count), **Subscription gains this week** (new, recovered, reactivated, resumed), and **Recent wins** (recovered revenue, subscriptions recovered, net active growth).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** no **Needs attention** banner, green trend badges on the **Store health** cards.
    </Check>

    <Note>
    **A failed payment is already being handled.** A recovery campaign opened the moment the charge failed: emails, retries on your schedule, and an automatic try of any backup card. [When a payment fails](/platform-v2/run/when-a-payment-fails) shows the timeline.
    </Note>

    <Note>
    **Home also nudges.** The setup checklist and recommendation cards are adoption prompts, not problems: **Hide setup** tucks the checklist behind a **Show setup** banner. On a young store, placeholders under **Recent wins** mean nightly analytics are still collecting.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Operational health">
    - Open **Settings > Operational health**: five panels, read top to bottom.

    | Panel | What it shows | Healthy looks like |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Alerts** | anything the platform wants you to know | **"Everything is running normally"** |
    | **Integration connections** | every connected provider, checked live against its own servers, plus the pinned Shopify versions | **Healthy** on checked rows, **Connected** where there's no live check, **Current** on the Shopify rows |
    | **Scheduled jobs** | every scheduled job with its last run | **OK** on every job (**Scheduled** with a next run is fine) |
    | **Webhook delivery (24h)** | Shopify's deliveries, counted by topic | ok counts, failed at zero |
    | **Queues & drains** | waiting work per queue, plus anything failed | numbers near zero |

    - Read any alert and click **Acknowledge** to mark it read; it resolves when the check behind it passes again. A quiet job raises an alert instead of keeping its **OK** badge.
    - Most mornings the screen is quiet, and that is the expected state.
    - Anything red or failing: [When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens) names every alert and its fix.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** no open alerts, providers **Healthy**, jobs **OK** or **Scheduled**, failed and waiting counts near zero.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/03-operational-health.png" alt="Operational health: alerts, integration connections, scheduled jobs, webhook delivery, and queues, all healthy." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Once a week, not every day">
    - Open **Analytics** and give three tabs a minute each: **Overview**, **Dunning**, **Cancellations**.
    - Pick a date range on any tab; **Overview** also takes a **Compare to** period.
    - **Dunning:** what failed and what recovery won back.
    - **Cancellations:** why subscribers leave and what your cancellation flow saves.
    - Two quirks: trend charts lag one nightly snapshot (headline numbers are live), and money is never summed across currencies.
    - Acting on the trends is territory for the [Growth Program](/growth-program/orientation).
  </Step>
</Steps>

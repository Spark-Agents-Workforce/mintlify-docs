---
title: "When emails don't arrive"
description: "Four causes explain nearly every missing or doubled email, and every fix is on two settings screens."
---

import { PlatformTag } from "/snippets/PlatformTag.jsx";

<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

**Settings > Integrations** decides who sends; **Settings > Notifications** decides what sends. Work the list in order; texts have their own page: [When texts don't arrive](/platform-v2/run/when-texts-dont-arrive).

<Steps>
  <Step title="No provider connected">
    - The symptom: nothing arrives on any channel, login codes included.
    - The platform never sends from its own servers: SendGrid or Klaviyo sends email, Twilio or Klaviyo texts; no provider, no messages.
    - In **Settings > Integrations**, connect a provider: credentials, settings, and **Test connection** live in its **Connect** modal (**Manage** once connected). First-time setup: [Set up email and SMS](/platform-v2/run/set-up-email-and-sms).
    - Under **Notification routing**, pick the **Email notifications** and **SMS notifications** routes, then **Save** in the bar at the top. SendGrid and Twilio send only once connected *and* selected; Klaviyo, once connected, receives every event whether or not it holds a route.

    <Warning>
    **Nothing waits for a provider.** With none connected, notifications are discarded, not queued (login codes included). Connecting starts delivery from that moment; nothing older comes back.
    </Warning>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/16-integrations.png" alt="Settings > Integrations: the three providers connected, with the Notification routing card below." width="560" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the test arrives (inbox, Klaviyo activity feed, or your phone).
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Klaviyo flows are still drafts">
    - The symptom: Klaviyo connected and routed, still nothing sends, login codes included.
    - Klaviyo flows import as drafts, and a draft flow sends nothing.
    - Open **Settings > Notifications > Email**, click **View created flows & templates** to see each flow's status, and set every flow you rely on to live in Klaviyo.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every flow you rely on reads live.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two emails for one event">
    - The symptom: subscribers get everything twice.
    - A live Klaviyo flow sends regardless of the email route, so a live flow plus an active SendGrid route is two senders for one event.
    - Pick one: set the flow back to draft in Klaviyo, or move the **Email notifications** route to Klaviyo under **Settings > Integrations**. The **Email** page flags the overlap.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/17-notification-routing.png" alt="The Notification routing card: one email route, one SMS route." width="640" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the **Email** page no longer flags an overlap.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The event is off, or the subscriber never opted in">
    - The symptom: one email never sends; the rest arrive.
    - A new install starts with every lifecycle event switched off.
    - Flip the event's switch under **Settings > Notifications > Email**. One subscriber missing texts is a consent case: [When texts don't arrive](/platform-v2/run/when-texts-dont-arrive).

    <Note>
    **Login codes always send.** **Portal login code** is marked always on; **Login link** shows a switch, but turning it off changes nothing. Missing login emails point to the first two causes, not a switch.
    </Note>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the event's switch is on.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the fix">
    - The subscriber's page in admin lists every send under **Notifications** with channel and status.
    - A bad API key retries a few times, then fails with the **Notification deliveries failed** alert. On **Settings > Operational health**, the **Integration connections** panel re-checks each key live: a bad one reads **Failing** with the provider's reason and recent delivery errors quoted, and **View logs** opens the provider's console.
    - The alert counts failures over a rolling 24 hours, so it can stay open up to a day after the fix; anything else there is [When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/18-notification-history.png" alt="A subscriber's Notifications table: each send with its channel, status, and engagement." width="640" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** new sends read sent; any open alert resolves within the day.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Still stuck? Read the provider's log">
    - "Sent" means your provider accepted the message; if it never lands, read the provider's dashboard.
    - Failed instead? Start with the row's own error text.

    | The message went through | Where the rest of the story lives |
    | --- | --- |
    | **SendGrid** | **Activity** in the SendGrid dashboard: search the subscriber's address. Bounces and blocks usually write back to the admin row; spam reports show only here. The feed holds about 3 days unless your plan includes Email Activity History. |
    | **Klaviyo** | Open the flow, then **Analytics > Recipient activity**, and search the subscriber's email: skipped or suppressed profiles state their reason. For one person across all flows, the profile's **Activity feed** is faster. |

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the provider's log names the reason.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

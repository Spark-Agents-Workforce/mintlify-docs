Nearly every notification problem, missing or doubled, has one of four causes, and every fix lives on two screens: **Settings > Integrations** decides who sends, **Settings > Notifications** decides what sends. Work the list in order; the first cause is the most common. Texts have their own page: [When texts don't arrive](/platform-v2/run/when-texts-dont-arrive).

<Steps>
  <Step title="No provider connected">
    - The symptom: nothing arrives on any channel. No automatic emails, no login codes, no texts.
    - The platform never sends from its own servers. SendGrid or Klaviyo sends your email; Twilio or Klaviyo sends your texts. Both send from accounts you own, so no provider means no messages.
    - In **Settings > Integrations**, connect a provider. Credentials, settings, and the test all live in the provider's **Connect** modal (**Manage** once connected). Send yourself a test with **Test connection**. First-time setup, end to end: [Set up email and SMS](/platform-v2/run/set-up-email-and-sms).
    - Back on **Settings > Integrations**, under **Notification routing**, pick the **Email notifications** and **SMS notifications** routes and **Save**. SendGrid and Twilio send only once connected *and* selected; Klaviyo, once connected, receives every event whether or not it holds a route. That difference drives the next two causes.

    <Warning>
    **Nothing waits for a provider.** With no provider connected, notifications are discarded, not queued (portal login codes included). Connecting starts delivery from that moment; nothing older comes back.
    </Warning>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/16-integrations.png" alt="Settings > Integrations: the three providers connected, with the Notification routing card below." width="560" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the test arrives: SendGrid's test in your inbox, Klaviyo's in the Klaviyo activity feed, Twilio's on your phone.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Klaviyo flows are still drafts">
    - The symptom: Klaviyo is connected and routed, and still nothing sends, login codes included.
    - Klaviyo flows import as drafts, and a draft flow sends nothing for its event.
    - Open **Settings > Notifications > Email** and click **View created flows & templates**: the list shows each flow's live-or-draft status and links you into it. In Klaviyo itself, set every flow you rely on to live.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every flow you rely on reads live, not draft.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two emails for one event">
    - The symptom: subscribers get everything twice.
    - A connected Klaviyo always receives events, and a live Klaviyo flow sends no matter which provider holds the email route. A live flow plus an active SendGrid route is two senders for one event.
    - Pick one: set the flow back to draft in Klaviyo, or, under **Settings > Integrations**, move the **Email notifications** route to Klaviyo. The **Email** page flags the overlap for you.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/17-notification-routing.png" alt="The Notification routing card: one email route, one SMS route." width="640" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the **Email** page no longer flags an overlap.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The event is off, or the subscriber never opted in">
    - The symptom: one specific email never sends while the rest arrive fine.
    - A new install starts quiet on purpose: every lifecycle event begins switched off.
    - Flip the event's switch under **Settings > Notifications > Email**; the page shows how many events are on. (One subscriber missing texts specifically is a consent case: [When texts don't arrive](/platform-v2/run/when-texts-dont-arrive).)

    <Note>
    **Login codes are never off.** The login code and login link always send and can't be disabled. If those are missing, the cause is one of the first two, not a toggle.
    </Note>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the event's switch is on.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the fix">
    - Open the subscriber's page in admin: the **Notifications** table lists every send with its channel and status.
    - Wrong API keys are never silent: a bad key retries a few times, then fails with the **Notification deliveries failed** alert.
    - Watch **Settings > Operational health**: that alert resolves on its own. It counts failures over a rolling 24 hours, so it can stay open up to a day after the fix while old ones age out. Anything else open there is [When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens).

    {/* shot:18 ui-admin "A subscriber's Notifications table: each send with its channel and status." */}

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** new sends read sent; any open alert resolves within the day.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Still stuck? Read the provider's log">
    - "Sent" in the admin means your provider accepted the message; delivering it is the provider's half. When a message reads sent but never lands, the rest of the story lives in the provider's own dashboard.
    - Failed in the admin's table instead? The row's own error text is the place to start; the provider log adds detail only when that text is vague.

    | The message went through | Where the rest of the story lives |
    | --- | --- |
    | **SendGrid** | The **Activity** page in the SendGrid dashboard: search the subscriber's address. Bounces and blocks usually also write themselves back into the admin row; spam reports show only here. |
    | **Klaviyo** | The flow's recipient activity in Klaviyo: a skipped or suppressed profile says why right there. |

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the provider's log names the reason, and it's theirs to explain, not the platform's.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

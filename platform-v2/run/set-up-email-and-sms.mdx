The platform never sends from its own servers: your providers do, from accounts you own. Connect and route them under **Settings > Integrations**, then turn on events under **Settings > Notifications** (also part of [Configure your store](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/configure-your-store)).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your providers">
    - Open **Settings > Integrations**. Under **Notification providers**, three can connect: **Klaviyo** (flows for email and SMS), **SendGrid** (email), **Twilio** (SMS). Helpdesk and analytics are covered in [Set up Gorgias](/platform-v2/run/set-up-gorgias) and [Set up analytics and monitoring](/platform-v2/run/set-up-analytics-and-monitoring).
    - Click **Connect** on each one you use; credentials, settings, and the test live in its modal (**Manage**, once connected). Fill SendGrid's **From email** and **From name** before testing; enter your own number on Twilio. Klaviyo's **Test connection** sends a test *event*, not a message.
    - On SendGrid, also click **Set up via API** under its event webhook to record opens, clicks, bounces, and blocks on the subscriber's **Notifications** table (greyed out until SendGrid is connected).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every provider shows its green Connected badge, and each test arrived.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/16-integrations.png" alt="Settings > Integrations: the three providers connected, with the Notification routing card below." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route the two channels">
    - Under **Notification routing**, pick the **Email notifications** and **SMS notifications** routes, then click **Save** in the bar at the top.
    - SendGrid and Twilio send only once connected *and* selected.
    - Klaviyo always receives events once connected, and live flows send regardless of the routes; a live flow plus an active SendGrid route means two emails for one event, so hold each event in one place.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** one route per channel, saved, no event in two senders.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/17-notification-routing.png" alt="The Notification routing card: one email route, one SMS route." width="640" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire Twilio's webhooks">
    - Using Twilio? Pick a **Messaging Service** or **From number** in its modal; two URLs show under **Webhooks: paste into your Twilio Messaging Service**.
    - Paste both into Twilio. **Incoming messages** makes the reply commands work (SKIP, PAUSE, RESUME, CANCEL, STOP/START); **Delivery status callback** is a backup, but paste it anyway.
    - Edit the automatic replies on the **SMS replies** page of the Notifications hub.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** both URLs pasted into the Messaging Service or number the modal saved.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/22-twilio-modal.png" alt="The Manage Twilio modal: credentials, the two webhook URLs to paste into Twilio, and the test send." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the events">
    - Open **Settings > Notifications**. A new install starts with every lifecycle event off.
    - The **Email** page lists each event with a switch and a template picker; the **SMS texts** page lists each text with a switch and an editable message.
    - The switches appear only while SendGrid is connected and govern only SendGrid; a Klaviyo event sends when its flow is live in Klaviyo.
    - First time on Klaviyo, click **Set up templates & flows**; flows arrive as drafts. Set the ones you rely on live in Klaviyo (**View created flows & templates** shows each status).
    - **Portal login code** and **Login link** have no working off switch; on Klaviyo, though, a draft flow sends nothing, login codes included.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the events you need are on: switches here, live flows in Klaviyo.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**When something goes quiet later**, the fixes live in [When emails don't arrive](/platform-v2/run/when-emails-dont-arrive) and [When texts don't arrive](/platform-v2/run/when-texts-dont-arrive).
</Note>

<Note>
**The templates and flows update with one click.** When newer versions ship, click **Update to latest** on the provider's card (an **Update available** badge appears): SendGrid templates get a new active version (the old stays in version history) and Klaviyo flows keep their live-or-draft status. Customized content gets replaced: re-apply your edits after.
</Note>

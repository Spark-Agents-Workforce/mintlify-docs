<span className="os-chip">2 min</span>

Almost anything a subscriber writes in about is self-serve in their portal at `/a/account` on your storefront, so the fastest reply is a login link, not doing it for them. It's also the commercially smart reply: a cancel in the portal walks through your cancellation flow, so your save offers show first. A cancel in your admin skips all of it.

|  | What gets done there |
|---|---|
| **Their portal** (send a login link) | Delay the next order, change its date or frequency, edit quantities, swap or add products, ship an order now, change addresses, add or switch cards, apply discount codes, cancel through your cancellation flow, reactivate. |
| **Your admin** ([do it yourself](/platform-v2/run/edit-one-subscription)) | The shipping price on a subscription, and most of the portal list when you'd rather fix it while they wait. Exceptions: cards (email only, below) and cancels (possible in admin, but it skips your cancellation flow). |
| **Shopify admin** | Refunds, returns, and edits to a placed order. Orders are Shopify orders, handled where they live. |

The portal row obeys your toggles: anything you switched off under **Customer portal features** in **Settings > Branding** moves to the **Your admin** row.

<Steps>
  <Step title="I can't log in">
    - The portal has no passwords; subscribers sign in with a single-use code or link. So "I can't log in" means one thing: send a fresh link.
    - Open **Subscribers**, open the person, and click **Magic Link**. **Copy login link** puts the link on your clipboard for your reply; **Email login link** sends it straight to them.
    - Either way it's single-use and expires in 60 minutes, so create it when you're ready to reply, not before.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the link is in your reply or their inbox; it signs them in without a password.
    </Check>

    {/* shot:08 ui-admin "The subscriber's page: the Magic Link menu open, with Copy login link and Email login link." */}

    <Warning>
    **Login emails need a working email provider.** **Email login link** and the portal's emailed sign-in codes only arrive if an email provider is connected and sending. (If Twilio sends your texts, the same sign-in code also goes by text when the subscriber has a phone on file.) Nothing landing? Start at [When emails don't arrive](/platform-v2/run/when-emails-dont-arrive), and use **Copy login link** in the meantime: paste works in any channel.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="My card needs updating">
    - Open the subscription (find it under **Subscriptions**, or come in through the subscriber's page), then click the pencil on the **Payment method** card. It holds exactly one action: **Send payment update email**.
    - Shopify sends the email and hosts the secure update page. Card numbers never pass through you, your admin, or the platform.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** email sent. The card stays between the subscriber and Shopify.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Anything else, one tap">
    - Any request you've built a quick action for becomes a single-use link that does it in one tap: skip, delay, ship now, reactivate with a discount, update payment (the same Shopify-hosted card page as above).
    - On the subscription's page, click **Quick action link** and pick one. The link is created for this subscriber alone and copied to your clipboard: paste it into your reply.
    - Single-use, expires in 60 minutes. On their side: one tap, a confirmation page, done.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the fix is in your reply, one tap on their side.
    </Check>

    {/* shot:09 ui-admin "The subscription's title bar: the Quick action link menu creating a single-use link for this subscriber." */}

    <Note>
    **The menu lists what you've built.** No **Quick action link** button on the page means no quick action is active yet. [Set up quick actions](/platform-v2/run/set-up-quick-actions) takes ten minutes; every active one appears in this menu.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**Some subscribers would rather text.** With Twilio connected, inbound texts handle the basics without you: `SKIP`, `PAUSE`, `RESUME`, `CANCEL` (the subscriber confirms before it runs), and `MANAGE` or `HELP` for the menu with their portal link. Turn any of the four commands (not the menu) off under **Settings > Notifications > SMS replies**.
</Tip>

title: "Edit one subscription"
description: "Find it, open it, change anything on it: date, products, address, discounts, status."
---

<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

<span className="os-chip">5 min</span>

Everything happens on one screen. Schedule and quantity edits collect in the save bar; everything else takes effect the moment you confirm.

| To change | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Next billing date, frequency | the **Schedule** card |
| Products, quantities, swaps | the **Items** card |
| Delivery address | the pencil on the **Customer** card |
| Discount codes | the **Payment summary** card |
| Shipping price | the pencil on the **Shipping method** card |
| Pause, cancel, ship now, merge | the title bar |
| A stuck payment (mid-campaign only) | the **Payment recovery** card ([When a payment fails](/platform-v2/run/when-a-payment-fails)) |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find it">
    - In the OpenSubs admin, open **Subscriptions**; search by subscriber name, email, or subscription number.
    - Narrow with the status filter (**All**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Cancelled**, **Failed**, **Billing issues**), or by **Product**, **Frequency**, or **Next charge date**.
    - Or open the person's page under **Subscribers**; every subscription they own is listed there.
    - Click the row to open it.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the subscription is open.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/04-subscriptions-list.png" alt="The subscriptions list: search, the status filter, and every subscription one click away." width="640" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the schedule">
    - In the **Schedule** card, pick a new **Next billing date**, a new **Frequency**, or both.
    - Nothing changes until you hit **Save**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/05-subscription-detail.png" alt="Subscription detail: schedule, items, and payment summary on one screen." width="560" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
    **There is no skip button, on purpose.** Pushing the next billing date one interval forward *is* the skip. Subscribers can skip themselves in their portal or by texting SKIP ([When a subscriber asks for help](/platform-v2/run/when-a-subscriber-asks)).
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the items">
    - In **Items**, edit a line's quantity; it rides the same save bar.
    - **Add product** opens your catalog: pick the product, set the quantity, and choose **Subscription** or **One-time** billing.
    - **Swap** replaces a line's product in one move; the remove icon deletes a line after you confirm.
    - You can't turn the last recurring line into a one-time item or remove the only line.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the saved lines match the next order.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/06-items-editor.png" alt="The items editor: quantities, swap, and add product on the subscription's lines." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change address, shipping, discounts">
    - The pencil on the **Customer** card opens **Edit shipping address**: pick a saved address with **Use this address**, add one with **Add new address**, or edit a saved one in place.
    - Editing a saved address warns **Update N subscriptions?**: **Save & update** re-applies it to every subscription shipping to that address and re-rates their shipping, so read the count.
    - The pencil on the **Shipping method** card: **Set custom price** pins a manual rate, and the button below returns it to your live rates (**Use automatic rate** while pinned, **Refresh rate** once automatic).
    - On the **Payment summary** card, enter a code in **Discount code** and click **Apply**. Codes stack; each has a **Remove** button, and **Remove all** clears them all.

    <Note>
    **Custom shipping prices exist only here.** The portal normally recalculates shipping when a subscriber edits; a pinned price stays put until you switch back to automatic.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship an order now">
    - Click **Ship now** in the title bar. The **Ship an order now** modal lists every line with a checkbox and a quantity; totals update as you tick.
    - Click **Charge & ship now**. The saved card is charged immediately, the order is created, and the next billing date advances one interval.

    <Warning>
    **No undo.** Once you confirm, the card is charged. The portal blocks a second same-day **Ship now**; the admin doesn't, so clicking twice charges twice.
    </Warning>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the new order is in the orders list.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/07-ship-now.png" alt="Ship an order now: per-line checkboxes, live totals, and the Charge & ship now confirm." width="480" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the status">
    - In the title bar: **Pause** stops billing instantly; **Resume** asks for the next billing date before anything restarts.
    - **Cancel subscription** asks for a cancellation reason (when configured); the cancellation stays in your records even if you later reactivate.
    - **Reactivate** starts a cancelled subscription again, next billing date of your choosing.

    <Note>
    **Paused cycles are skipped, never made up.** Billing restarts on the date you pick at **Resume** (it suggests tomorrow); nothing is owed for the gap. An admin pause runs until you resume it; portal and cancellation-flow pauses end on their own, onto the date the original schedule would bill next. A paused subscriber's portal shows no next order date, though your admin still shows one.
    </Note>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the status badge says what you meant.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge and split">
    - **Merge subscriptions** appears only on an active subscription whose subscriber has other active ones. The ones you pick merge in: recurring items move over, they stop billing, and one-time and gift items stay behind.
    - Merged-away subscriptions end as expired, not cancelled, so they never count as churn; expired subscriptions are hidden from the list.
    - To split: with two or more recurring lines, **Move to new subscription** on a line opens **Move items to a new subscription**: tick the lines to move, set the next order date, keep or change the frequency. Same card, address, and pricing; one-time and gift lines can't move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the history">
    - The **History** card lists every change, day by day; rows expand, and order rows link to their orders.
    - Every entry names its actor: **Customer** (including SMS actions), **Merchant**, **Quick action**, **Gorgias** ([Set up Gorgias](/platform-v2/run/set-up-gorgias)), plus automated **Billing**, **Recovery**, **Flow**, and **System**.
    - The store-wide version lives at **Analytics > Activity**, with source filters and a CSV export.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the change has a row naming who.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**What isn't here, on purpose.** No card entry (the payment-update email handles it: [When a subscriber asks for help](/platform-v2/run/when-a-subscriber-asks)), no manual payment retry (the recovery schedule owns it: [When a payment fails](/platform-v2/run/when-a-payment-fails)), and no delete: cancel is as far as it goes.
</Note>

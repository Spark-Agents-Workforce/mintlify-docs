---

When a subscriber cancels in their portal, the flow runs first: a reason, then an offer matched to it, then the cancel. A cancel done in the admin skips the offer entirely ([When a subscriber asks for help](/platform-v2/run/when-a-subscriber-asks)).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the flow">
    - Open **Settings > Cancellation flows** and create a flow. The builder has four stages: **Name**, **Add reasons**, **Configure offers**, **Review and publish**.
    - Reasons are the choices a cancelling subscriber picks from ("Too expensive", "Too much product"). Their on-screen order is randomized, so there's no reorder control and no first-position bias.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every reason a subscriber might have is on the list.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/30-flow-builder.png" alt="The cancellation flow builder: the flow's reasons with their offers, stage by stage." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the offers">
    - Each reason can carry **rules**: extra offers aimed at a segment. The first matching rule wins; the default offer catches everyone else.
    - Offers are the saves: a discount, a pause, a delay, a frequency change, a merge. Match them to the reason: "too expensive" wants a discount; "too much product" wants a delay or lower frequency.
    - Follow-up offers give you a second swing after a declined first offer.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every reason lands on an offer that answers it.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish, and know the switch">
    - **Save draft** saves edits without changing status: on an unpublished flow they stay private. On a published flow, **Save draft** applies your edits to the live flow immediately. **Publish flow** makes an unpublished flow live once the readiness checklist passes.
    - There is no separate on/off switch: publishing a flow turns the feature on; deactivating your last flow turns it off.
    - One flow is the active default per store; targeted flows can sit alongside it.
    - **Cooldown (days)**, on the same **Settings > Cancellation flows** screen, suppresses repeat offers to a subscriber who recently took one: default 60, 0 turns it off.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the flow reads published and a test cancel walks through it.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune it from the numbers">
    - **Analytics > Cancellations** shows the funnel: flow started, reason selected, offer shown, offer accepted, offer declined, abandoned, cancelled. Many **Offer shown** with few **Offer accepted** is an offer problem; a reason nobody picks is a wording problem.
    - Offers that don't fire are usually gating, not the flow: [When a save offer doesn't show](/platform-v2/run/when-a-save-offer-doesnt-show).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** you know your save rate.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Only the portal runs the flow.** An admin cancel skips every offer; the fastest reply to "please cancel me" is a login link.
</Note>

---
title: "When a payment fails"
description: "Retries, emails, and the backup card run by themselves. Watch it happen; step in rarely."
---

import { PlatformTag } from "/snippets/PlatformTag.jsx";

<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

A failed charge opens a recovery campaign that runs itself. Step in only when a card never gets updated, a bank wants a confirmation, or a product is out of stock.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Let it run">
    The response, in order:

    | Moment | What happens by itself |
    | --- | --- |
    | The charge fails | Recorded on the subscription; a recovery campaign opens. |
    | Right away | The subscriber gets the first email, with a secure card-update link. A backup card on file is tried: one try per failed attempt on the primary. |
    | On your schedule | Each step runs on its day and retries the charge, sends a reminder, or both. |
    | After the last step | The ending you chose: cancel (the default), pause, or leave active and stop retrying. |

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** nothing above needed you.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it happen">
    - **Subscriptions**, filtered to **Billing issues**, lists everyone in recovery; Home's **Needs attention** banner's **Review subscriptions** button lands on the same list.
    - Two filter names look alike and aren't:

    | Filter | Who's in it |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Billing issues** | Not cancelled or expired, and the last billing attempt errored, whatever the status badge says. The set Home counts. |
    | **Failed** | Subscriptions whose contract status is Failed, mirrored from Shopify. Recovery never sets it. |

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/13-billing-issues.png" alt="The Billing issues filter: every subscription in recovery, with the failure reason as a badge." width="640" />
    </Frame>

    - Open one: the **Payment recovery** card shows an **In recovery** badge, the amount at risk, retries used, the last error, and the dunning schedule with steps marked **Done**, **Next**, or **Upcoming**. Once the schedule is spent, **Next retry** becomes **Final action** with the ending's date. An out-of-stock campaign shows an **Out of stock** badge and no schedule. The card exists only mid-campaign.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/35-recovery-card.png" alt="The Payment recovery card: amount at risk, retries used, next retry, last error, and the dunning schedule with Done, Next, and Upcoming steps." width="640" />
    </Frame>

    - Below it, **Billing history** lists one row per attempt, **Paid** or **Failed**, with a **Recovery** badge on scheduled retries and **Backup card** on backup tries; failed amounts are labeled "attempted", not charged.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/14-billing-history.png" alt="Billing history: the attempt ledger, a Paid row linking to its order." width="640" />
    </Frame>

    - **Analytics > Dunning** totals what failed and what the schedule won back.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every attempt is on the screen.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune the schedule, once">
    - The whole campaign (retry days, emails, the ending) lives at **Settings > Payment recovery**; [Set up payment recovery](/platform-v2/run/set-up-payment-recovery) walks every field.
    - **After the last step** decides the ending.
    - **Out-of-stock retries** run their own quieter schedule, with no emails to the subscriber.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the schedule matches how hard you want to chase.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The subscriber who never fixes the card">
    - If a campaign drags on, nudge once: click the pencil on the **Payment method** card and choose **Send payment update email**, or paste a **Quick action link** into a personal reply ([When a subscriber asks for help](/platform-v2/run/when-a-subscriber-asks)).
    - Either link signs the subscriber into their portal's payment section to pick another saved card or add one. OpenSubs never stores card details: they're exchanged for a Shopify vault token and discarded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The bank that wants a confirmation">
    - Sometimes the bank makes the subscriber confirm the charge (3D Secure): the bank-authentication email carries the bank's confirmation link, the campaign waits, and the **Payment recovery** card's next retry reads **Waiting for customer authentication**.
    - It waits as long as it takes; point a confused subscriber back at that email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The product that's out of stock">
    - A renewal that fails because your product is out of stock never emails the subscriber.
    - A partly stocked renewal bills without the missing line; the subscription keeps every line for the next cycle.
    - With every line out of stock, the charge waits on the **Out-of-stock retries** schedule. Restock in Shopify and the next retry bills as normal.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** restocking is the whole fix.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="End a campaign on purpose">
    A campaign closes in one of four ways; you control three:

    | How it ends | What closes it |
    | --- | --- |
    | The payment succeeds | A scheduled retry that lands (primary, backup, or fixed card) closes the campaign on the spot, as recovered. A **Ship now** payment also ends the chase; the campaign closes at its next scheduled retry. |
    | You click **Stop recovery** | The button on the **Payment recovery** card. Retries and reminder emails stop immediately, the **After the last step** ending never fires, and the subscription doesn't change. |
    | The schedule runs out | The last step fails, one more retry gap passes, and the **After the last step** ending fires. |
    | The subscription stops | Cancel or pause it and the campaign is abandoned at the next scheduled retry: no failure email, no ending action. |

    - Want the order out today? **Ship now**, then **Charge & ship now**.
    - Want just the chasing to stop? **Stop recovery**. The missed order is never billed and nothing bills until you move the next billing date or use **Ship now**; it stays in **Billing issues** until a charge succeeds, and a later failed cycle opens a fresh campaign.
    - Want the subscription ended too? Cancel or pause it.
    - Want future campaigns shorter? Cut steps on **Settings > Payment recovery**; changes apply to running campaigns too.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** pay it, stop it, outlast it, or end the subscription under it.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**There is no retry button, on purpose.** The schedule owns retries. When a fixed card needs the order today: **Ship now**, then **Charge & ship now** ([Edit one subscription](/platform-v2/run/edit-one-subscription)).
</Note>

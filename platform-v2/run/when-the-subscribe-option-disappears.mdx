---
title: "When the subscribe option disappears"
description: "A theme change can silently drop the widget, and every new sale becomes one-time. Two checks find it."
---

<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

The subscription widget lives inside your published Shopify theme. A theme swap, duplicate, or big update can quietly drop it while the product page keeps selling everything as one-time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check placement on Extensions">
    - Open **Settings > Extensions**: a row per surface, each with its placement status and a button into the right editor (theme editor for the product page, checkout editor for the rest).
    - If the widget is missing, follow that button and add the block back to the product page, the same move as go-live's [The swap](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/the-swap).
    - The product-page row reports **Enabled** or **Not enabled**: from Shopify when opened inside the Shopify admin, otherwise from a check of your published theme (**Unknown** means that check couldn't run). The checkout and customer-account rows report **Placed** or **Not placed** inside the Shopify admin; outside it they fall back to activity, where "No activity yet" means untested, not necessarily unplaced.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the product page shows the subscription option again, in a private window too.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/33-extensions.png" alt="Settings > Extensions: each surface with its placement status and a button into its editor." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The widget is there but looks broken">
    - A widget that renders unstyled, or where every add lands as one-time, usually means the storefront is serving leftover development assets.
    - That belongs to whoever runs your code: a redeploy of the extensions fixes it; theme editing can't reach it.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** styled widget, and a test add lands as a subscription.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**After any theme work, buy once.** Open a product page, add the subscription option to cart, and confirm the cart says subscription: the same proof your go-live used ([Prove it with real money](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/prove-it-with-real-money)).
</Note>

The subscription widget lives inside your published Shopify theme. Swap themes, duplicate one and publish it, or take a big theme update, and the widget can quietly vanish, while the product page keeps selling everything as one-time. No error anywhere; just a revenue line bending down. Two checks find it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check placement on Extensions">
    - Open **Settings > Extensions**: a row per surface, storefront widget included, each with its placement status and (where placing is your job) a button into the right editor (theme editor for the product page, checkout editor for the rest).
    - Widget missing after a theme change? Follow that button and add the block back to the product page, the same move as go-live's [The swap](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/the-swap).
    - Read the statuses knowing where they come from: opened inside the Shopify admin, each surface reports **Placed** or **Not placed** directly. Opened outside it, the page falls back to activity heartbeats, where "No activity yet" means untested, not necessarily unplaced.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the product page shows the subscription option again, in a private window too.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/33-extensions.png" alt="Settings > Extensions: each surface with its placement status and a button into its editor." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The widget is there but looks broken">
    - A widget that renders unstyled, or where every add lands as one-time, usually means the storefront is serving leftover development assets from an interrupted engineering session.
    - That one belongs to whoever runs your code: a redeploy of the extensions fixes it, and it isn't something theme editing can reach.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** styled widget, and a test add lands as a subscription, not a one-time.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**After any theme work, buy once.** The sixty-second habit that catches all of this: open a product page, add the subscription option to cart, and confirm the cart says subscription. The same proof-by-purchase your go-live used ([Prove it with real money](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/prove-it-with-real-money)).
</Note>

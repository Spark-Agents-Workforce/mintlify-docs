Orders belong to Shopify; the subscription belongs to the platform. Neither cancels the other, in either direction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refund or cancel the order, in Shopify">
    - Start from the subscription: the **Summary** card's **Linked orders** list deep-links every order into the Shopify admin; the order that started the subscription carries an **Origin** badge.
    - Refund, partially refund, or cancel it there, like any Shopify order. The platform mirrors the numbers and changes nothing else.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the refund is done in Shopify.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/27-linked-orders.png" alt="The Summary card's linked orders, each deep-linking into Shopify, the origin order badged." width="360" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know what didn't happen">
    Nothing else moves. Surface by surface:

    | Where you look | After the refund |
    | --- | --- |
    | The subscription | Unchanged: same status, next billing date unmoved, nothing paused, no email sent. |
    | **Billing history** | Still reads **Paid**: the charge did happen; the refund lives on the order in Shopify. |
    | The subscriber's portal | Shows **Refunded** or **Partially Refunded** on their order history and the order's page (a cancelled order reads Refunded or Voided). |
    | **Analytics** | Overview revenue is net of refunds, and cancelled orders drop out; the Revenue tab's category breakdown classifies categories before refunds. |

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the status badge, next billing date, and **Billing history** read exactly as before.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="If the subscriber is done, cancel the subscription too">
    - A refund over one bad box may need no second move: the schedule keeps rolling.
    - When they're done, cancel the subscription as its own step: **Cancel subscription** in the title bar ([Edit one subscription](/platform-v2/run/edit-one-subscription)), or better, send a login link so they cancel in the portal and your cancellation flow gets its chance ([When a subscriber asks for help](/platform-v2/run/when-a-subscriber-asks)).
    - In reverse: cancelling a subscription refunds nothing by itself; money already charged stays charged until you refund it in Shopify.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** refunded in Shopify, cancelled here.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**A refund doesn't rewind rewards.** The refunded order still counts toward reward progress, and a gift that shipped on it stays **Processed** ([When a reward goes missing](/platform-v2/run/when-a-reward-goes-missing)).
</Note>

<Note>
**One order can belong to several subscriptions.** Sibling subscriptions due the same day, on the same card, address, and currency, bill as one combined order: one charge row on the base subscription, and the order linked from all of them.
</Note>

Two gates decide whether a cancelling subscriber sees an offer. Check both, then rule out the look-alike.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The cooldown already spent this subscriber">
    - A subscriber who took a save incentive recently isn't offered another: the cooldown (default 60 days) is enforced server-side and holds across flow edits.
    - A blocked subscriber falls back to the flow's non-incentive path.
    - Change it in **Cooldown (days)** on **Settings > Cancellation flows**; 0 turns it off.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** you've checked this subscriber against the cooldown window.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The offer's portal feature is switched off">
    - An offer can only run what the portal allows: a pause offer needs **Pause subscription**, a merge offer **Merge subscriptions**, a delay offer (alone or with a discount) **Delay next order**, a frequency offer **Change delivery frequency**, all under **Customer portal features** ([What subscribers can do for themselves](/platform-v2/run/what-subscribers-can-do-themselves)).
    - A gated offer is skipped silently: the subscriber goes to your fallback offer if it passes the same gates, otherwise to the plain cancel confirmation. Follow-up offers only enter after an explicit decline and are gated the same way.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every offer type in your flow has its matching portal feature on.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The look-alike: a taken discount that later disappears">
    - An accepted discount save applies for a limited number of billing cycles, one by default; once used, it drops off the subscription's summary.
    - That's the offer being spent, not a gate failing. If they cancel again soon, the cooldown in the first step governs what they see.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the "missing" discount ran its cycle and left.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the funnel, not the anecdote">
    - **Analytics > Cancellations** shows the whole path: flow started, reason selected, offer shown, offer accepted, offer declined, abandoned, cancelled.
    - Offers that show but never get accepted are an offer problem: back to [Set up your cancellation flow](/platform-v2/run/set-up-your-cancellation-flow).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** **Offer shown** sits close to **Reason selected** in the funnel.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

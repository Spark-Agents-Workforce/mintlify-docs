Payment recovery is the machinery behind [When a payment fails](/platform-v2/run/when-a-payment-fails): the retries, the dunning emails, the ending. It ships with a sane default schedule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shape the schedule">
    - Open **Settings > Payment recovery**. The schedule is a list of steps, each running on its day, counted from the first failure.
    - Each step carries its **Day**, a **Retry charge** switch, an email to send (**First failure**, **Retry reminder**, **Second reminder**, **Final notice**, or **Off**), and a **Remove** control.
    - An email-only step sends on its day. A step that also retries sends its email only if the retry fails.
    - Day 0 is the exception: the charge already failed, so it only sends an email and its retry switch is greyed out.
    - **Add step** to chase longer; remove steps to chase less.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the days climb from top to bottom.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/15-payment-recovery.png" alt="Payment recovery: the step schedule with retry switches and emails, and the after-the-last-step action." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the ending">
    - **After the last step** decides what happens when the schedule runs out: cancel the subscription (the default), pause it, or stop retrying and leave it active.
    - The ending waits a few days after the final step, so the last email can land.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** **After the last step** shows the ending you want.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The two extras">
    - **Out-of-stock retries** are three fields: **Retry attempts**, **Days between retries**, and their own ending, **If still out of stock after all retries**, defaulting to **Stop retrying (leave the subscription active)**. No emails go to the subscriber.
    - **Other emails** holds two switches: **Bank authentication (3D Secure)**, on by default, and **Payment recovered**, off by default.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the switches and out-of-stock fields are set.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    - Click **Save** in the bar at the top of the page; nothing applies until you do.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the save bar is gone.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Most stores set this once and never return.** Saved edits take effect immediately, including for campaigns already in flight. The day-to-day view is [When a payment fails](/platform-v2/run/when-a-payment-fails), including each in-recovery subscription's **Payment recovery** card with its **Stop recovery** button.
</Note>

Three optional connections on **Settings > Integrations** watch instead of send: **Triple Whale** receives subscription events for attribution, **PostHog** shows portal usage, and **Sentry** reports portal errors. Connect each with a key from your own account; the senders (Klaviyo, SendGrid, Twilio) have [their own page](/platform-v2/run/set-up-email-and-sms).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Forward subscription events to Triple Whale">
    - Open **Settings > Integrations**, find **Triple Whale** under **Analytics providers**, and click **Connect**.
    - Paste an API key created in Triple Whale under **Settings > API Keys** with the data-in scope, then click **Connect**.
    - Every subscription change then flows by itself: created, active, paused, cancelled, with the cancellation reason.
    - No test button; proof is data arriving after the next subscription event. Disconnecting stops it.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the Triple Whale card reads Connected.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch portal usage in PostHog">
    - Find **PostHog** under **Analytics providers** and click **Connect**.
    - **Project API key** is the `phc_` key from PostHog under **Settings > Project**. **API host** stays blank for PostHog Cloud US; `https://eu.i.posthog.com` for EU Cloud, or your self-hosted address.
    - Click **Connect**, then **Test connection**: it captures an **OpenSubs Test Event** you can watch arrive in PostHog.
    - Portal visits are tracked like any site, subscription lifecycle events are captured server-side, and after login both land on one PostHog person, keyed by email.
    - Only the portal is tracked, never your admin; it loads its own PostHog instance, separate from any in your storefront theme.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/37-posthog-modal.png" alt="The PostHog modal: the project API key, the optional API host, and the test that captures an OpenSubs Test Event." width="560" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the test event shows in PostHog.
    </Check>

    <Note>
    **Events arrive scrubbed.** Login codes and click-to-act links are stripped before events leave for PostHog.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send portal errors to Sentry">
    - Find **Sentry** under **Monitoring** and click **Connect**.
    - **DSN** comes from your Sentry project under **Settings > Client Keys (DSN)**. **Environment** is optional and tags every report; `production` is the usual choice.
    - Click **Connect**, then **Send test event**: a real event lands in your Sentry **Issues** feed.
    - Portal errors then report themselves as they happen: browser crashes and server-route failures, tagged with your shop.
    - The scope is the portal only: your admin is not tracked, and storefront theme errors are filtered out.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/38-sentry-modal.png" alt="The Sentry modal: the DSN, the optional environment tag, and the test that sends a real event to the Issues feed." width="560" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the test event is in your Sentry Issues feed.
    </Check>

    <Note>
    **This watches your subscribers' side, not the machine.** Monitoring for the admin, background jobs, and webhooks is a deployment-level setting for whoever runs your code; the platform also watches itself: [When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens).
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Operational health keeps an eye on all three.** Each card gets a row under **Integration connections** on **Settings > Operational health**: PostHog and Sentry are re-verified live, Triple Whale shows Connected only, and a failing row names the reason. The panel is part of [the morning check](/platform-v2/run/the-morning-check).
</Note>

<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

<span className="os-chip">one sitting</span>

Discontinuing a product that live subscriptions still contain is safe in one order: move the subscribers, verify zero, then delete.

<Warning>
**Delete nothing until the count reads zero.** The admin won't stop you; the delete confirm only warns about *new* subscribers.
</Warning>

| If you delete | What breaks, quietly |
| --- | --- |
| The product or its variants | Renewals can fail at the charge and get treated like card problems: recovery campaigns open and dunning emails go out ([When a payment fails](/platform-v2/run/when-a-payment-fails)). |
| Just its selling plan group | Billing continues, but the line reads "Currently unavailable", reminder emails drop it from the order total, and frequency and variant editing disappears. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Count who's still on it">
    - Open **Bulk operations** > **New bulk operation** and keep the preset condition: **Subscription status** is **Active**.
    - **Add condition**, set the field to **Contains products**, and pick the retiring product. The estimate is your number; **Download CSV** lists the names.
    - The **Product** filter on **Subscriptions** reads higher: it also counts one-time lines and past statuses.
    - Do this while the selling plan group still exists: the filter and pickers only list attached products.
    - One-time add-on lines don't count in the estimate.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** you have the number and the CSV of names.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/25-contains-products.png" alt="The audience: a Contains products condition on the retiring product, the estimate counting who's still on it." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the substitute to a selling plan group">
    - Swapping to a replacement product? Attach it to a live selling plan group under **Settings > Selling plans** first.
    - The swap prices the new line from that group, so check it covers the swap-target variant; uncovered means full catalog price, no subscription discount, and an unavailable line.
    - Removing the product outright? Skip this step.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the substitute has a plan attached, swap-target variant covered.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap or remove, in one run">
    - In the same bulk operation, add the action. **Swap product variant** takes two picks: **Replace** the retiring variant, **with** the substitute. **Remove product** takes one: the retiring variant.
    - Both are variant-scoped: one action per variant, up to four per run.
    - Both act on active subscriptions only and send no notification; send any heads-up yourself.
    - **Remove product** refuses to empty a subscription: anyone whose only recurring line is the retiring product lands in the **Skipped** tab.
    - Run it; [Change many at once](/platform-v2/run/change-many-at-once) covers the builder.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the run finished, every skipped row read.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/26-swap-action.png" alt="The Swap product variant action: Replace the retiring variant, with the substitute." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle the subscriptions the run skipped">
    - Single-line subscriptions that Remove skipped: swap them to the substitute, edit them [one at a time](/platform-v2/run/edit-one-subscription), or run a bulk **Cancel subscription**.
    - Paused and failed subscriptions are outside both actions' reach; handle them from their subscription pages.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the skipped list is empty, or every name has a decision.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify zero, then retire it">
    - Build the same audience in a new bulk operation and confirm **0 subscriptions match**.
    - Then set the product to draft or archived (or delete it) in the Shopify admin, and delete its group under **Settings > Selling plans**.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** zero first, Shopify second.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Just stopping new subscribers is gentler than retirement.** Remove the product from your Online Store sales channel in Shopify, leaving its status active: existing subscriptions keep billing, editing, and shipping. The **One-time only** purchase type under **Settings > Selling plans** stops portal visitors adding it as a new subscription line.
</Note>

<Note>
**Same screen, different problem: stale product data.** If the portal shows an old price, a dead variant, or a missing image, select the products in the **Products** view of **Settings > Selling plans** and click **Refresh selected** (or **Refresh all from Shopify**).
</Note>

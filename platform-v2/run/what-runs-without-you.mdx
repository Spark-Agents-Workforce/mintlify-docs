From launch day to every ordinary day:

- **Outcome:** you know what the platform does on its own, and the two screens that prove it did.
- **Time:** a read, about 5 minutes.
- **Before you start:** [Live for New Orders](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/what-today-changes) is behind you, and if you switched platforms, [Migrate](/platform-v2/migrate/what-actually-moves) too.
- **You'll touch:** the OpenSubs admin. Most days, nothing else.

Running a subscription platform sounds like a job. It's a glance. Everything below fires on a schedule inside your own Supabase project (no vendor in the loop, nothing extra to keep running), and every run is recorded, so a quiet screen means "checked and fine", not "hopefully fine".

| When | What runs by itself |
| --- | --- |
| Every minute | Queued emails and texts go out. Imports, bulk edits, and queued Shopify changes advance. |
| Every 15 minutes | A watchdog checks every job and queue, and opens an alert on **Settings > Operational health** when something stalls or fails. |
| Every hour | **Billing.** Each due subscription is charged after your store's clock passes the **Daily billing time** you chose in **Settings > Billing**: the hour is a floor, not a slot, so a subscription that comes due later in the day still bills the same day. Timed pauses resume when they end; failed payments get their scheduled retries. |
| Overnight | Shipment reminders queue up, analytics snapshots run, multi-currency display prices re-sync from Shopify, old records get cleaned out. |

A missed hour catches up on the next one, and a caught-up run can't double-charge: each due charge is picked up exactly once, no matter how many runs see it.

Which leaves you two screens:

- **Home** greets you with a **Needs attention** banner only when something needs you: failed payments, or an open alert. No banner, nothing needed.
- **Settings > Operational health** is where the machine reports: scheduled jobs, webhook delivery, queue health, and any alerts. All of it re-checked every 15 minutes.

Most blips fix themselves before you see them. Failed payments follow a recovery schedule you control. Shopify redelivers webhooks that didn't land. Queued work retries until it clears. The alerts that do open are the small set of things that genuinely want a human, and [When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens) matches each one to its fix.

So this group is organized by feature, and the sidebar shows it: each feature keeps its setup guide and its what-went-wrong pages side by side. [The morning check](/platform-v2/run/the-morning-check) is still the whole job most days: five minutes on those two screens, with its triage partner right below it in the sidebar. Around it sit [subscriptions](/platform-v2/run/edit-one-subscription) and the requests that follow them, [quick actions](/platform-v2/run/set-up-quick-actions), [bulk operations](/platform-v2/run/change-many-at-once), [payment recovery](/platform-v2/run/set-up-payment-recovery), [email and SMS](/platform-v2/run/set-up-email-and-sms), and [rewards](/platform-v2/run/set-up-rewards). The one recurring chore is [updates](/platform-v2/run/update-the-platform), and [where your job ends](/platform-v2/run/your-job-and-when-to-get-help) closes the group.

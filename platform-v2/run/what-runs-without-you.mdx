- **Outcome:** you know what runs on its own, and the two screens that prove it.
- **Time:** a read, about 5 minutes.
- **Before you start:** [Live for New Orders](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/what-today-changes) is behind you; if you switched platforms, [Migrate](/platform-v2/migrate/what-actually-moves) too.
- **You'll touch:** the OpenSubs admin.

Every schedule below lives inside your own Supabase project, no vendor in the loop. Some work runs in your own app; if that app is down, those queues wait and drain when it's back. Every run is recorded.

| When | What runs by itself |
| --- | --- |
| Every minute | Queued emails and texts go out. Imports, bulk edits, and queued Shopify changes advance. |
| Every 15 minutes | A watchdog checks every job and queue, and opens an alert on **Settings > Operational health** when something stalls or fails. |
| Every hour | **Billing.** Each due subscription is charged after your store's clock passes the **Daily billing time** you chose in **Settings > Billing**. That time is a start, not a deadline: billing stays open for the rest of the day, so a subscription that comes due later still bills the same day. Timed pauses resume when they end; failed payments get their scheduled retries. |
| Overnight | Shipment reminders queue up, analytics snapshots run, multi-currency display prices re-sync from Shopify, and old records get cleaned out (some cleanups weekly). These run at fixed UTC times, so "overnight" is literal only for stores near UTC. |

A missed hour catches up on the next one; each due charge is picked up exactly once, so nothing double-charges.

Your two screens:

- **Home** shows a **Needs attention** banner only when something needs you: failed payments, or an open alert.
- **Settings > Operational health** shows alerts, connected providers checked live against their own servers, scheduled jobs, webhook delivery, and queue health. Alert checks re-run every 15 minutes; the panels read live when you open the page.

Most blips fix themselves: failed payments follow a recovery schedule you control, Shopify redelivers webhooks that didn't land, and queued work retries until it clears. [When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens) matches each alert to its fix.

The group is organized by feature, each setup guide beside its what-went-wrong pages. [The morning check](/platform-v2/run/the-morning-check) is the whole job most days, with [When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens) directly beneath it. The one recurring chore, [updates](/platform-v2/run/update-the-platform), closes the group.

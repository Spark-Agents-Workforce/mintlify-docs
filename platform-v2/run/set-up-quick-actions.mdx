<span className="os-chip">10 min</span>

A quick action is a link that performs one change on a subscription when the subscriber taps it: no login, one confirmation page. Build them once; use them from any subscription page or as merge tags in notification emails.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the action">
    - Open **Settings > Quick actions** and click **Create quick action**.
    - Give it a **Name**, then pick the **Action type**:

    | Family | Action types |
    | --- | --- |
    | Schedule | **Skip next order** · **Delay next order** · **Ship now** |
    | Status | **Pause subscription** · **Resume subscription** · **Reactivate subscription** · **Reactivate with a discount** · **Reactivate with a one-time add-on** |
    | Changes | **Apply a discount code** · **Add a product** · **Swap a product** · **Change delivery frequency** · **Update payment method** |
    | Combined | **Multiple actions**: two to four steps run in order, each type once, reactivation first when present |
    | Special | **Convert order to subscription**: acts on a past one-time order, with an optional discount code |

    - Types that need details (discount code, product and variant, new frequency) ask for them right there.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the action does one clear thing and its name says so.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/29-quick-actions.png" alt="Settings > Quick actions: the list of actions with their status badges." width="640" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set its availability">
    - Under **Availability**: **Status** (**Active**, **Paused**, or **Archived**), **Max uses per subscription** (empty means unlimited), and **Expires**.
    - Empty **Expires** means never; a date ends at 11:59 PM that day. Individual links have shorter lifetimes (below).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** status reads **Active**, with the limits you mean.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it to work, two ways">
    - **In your replies:** every active action appears in the **Quick action link** menu on a subscription's page; a click copies a single-use, 60-minute link ([When a subscriber asks for help](/platform-v2/run/when-a-subscriber-asks)).
    - **In your notifications:** after the first save, a **Notifications** section appears; tick the sends under **Add this link to notification sends**, or copy its **Merge tag** into a template.
    - Links inside notification sends are reusable for that send's life (about 30 days).
    - The classic: a dunning email with a one-tap **Update payment method** link; its confirmation page drops the subscriber on their subscription in the portal, signed in.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the action shows in the **Quick action link** menu and the sends you picked.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**A quick action link is a key, scoped to one subscriber.** Anyone holding a live link can use it, so treat it like a signed-in session. Admin-copied links work once and die in an hour; notification-email links last the send's life, so keep them out of anywhere public.
</Note>

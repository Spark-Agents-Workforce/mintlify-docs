Each tier is earned once a customer has placed a set number of recurring orders. Gifts wait in their portal to redeem; discounts apply to the next order automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn the program on">
    - Open **Settings > Rewards** and flip **Enable rewards**, in the **Rewards program** panel on the right.
    - Left off, the page warns that tiers won't be earned until the program is on.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the program is on, or deliberately off while you build tiers first.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/28-rewards-settings.png" alt="Settings > Rewards: the program switch, reward tiers with their rewards, and the reset section below." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the tiers">
    - Click **Add tier** and set its earn point: **Earned after N recurring orders**.
    - **Add reward** fills the tier. Three kinds:

    | Reward type | What the subscriber gets |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Free gift** | A product you pick (**Choose gift**), added free to their next order once redeemed. |
    | **Amount off** | A fixed amount off their next order, applied automatically. |
    | **Percentage off** | A percentage off their next order, applied automatically. |

    - A tier can hold any number of free gifts but only one discount.
    - Pick gifts from inside the Shopify admin; opened anywhere else, **Choose gift** only tells you the picker needs the Shopify admin.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every gift resolves to a real product.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, then watch it work">
    - **Save.** The banner confirms: "Reward settings saved."
    - **View analytics** in the title bar opens **Analytics > Rewards**: processed versus claimed and top gifts.
    - Per subscriber, earned rewards live on the subscription page's **Reward history** card and available gifts in **Available rewards** ([When a reward goes missing](/platform-v2/run/when-a-reward-goes-missing)).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the first earned reward shows up in Reward history.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**The one dangerous control here is Reset all order counts**, under **Reset reward progress**. It sets every customer's recurring-order count back to zero and can't be undone; the tiers stay. Use it for relaunches, not tidying.
</Warning>

Three screens make the portal look and sound like your store: branding, translations, FAQ.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Brand it">
    - **Settings > Branding** holds the look: logo, colors, and theme for the customer account area, with **Customer portal features** lower on the page ([What subscribers can do for themselves](/platform-v2/run/what-subscribers-can-do-themselves)).
    - The live preview shows changes as you make them, desktop and mobile.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/41-branding-preview.png" alt="Settings > Branding: theme presets and logo on the left, the live portal preview with sample data on the right." width="640" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the preview looks like your store, both sizes.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reword it, in any language">
    - **Settings > Translations** lists every phrase the portal can say, editable per language and searchable.
    - Edit the English row to reword the portal store-wide; every other language layers on top.
    - A blank field falls back to the default wording; each language saves as a whole.
    - A draft language never serves to subscribers; its **Preview** link is the only place it renders until you set it live.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the phrases subscribers see most read like you.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the FAQ">
    - **Settings > FAQ** holds the portal's help content in sections of questions and answers; the portal shows no FAQ item until you add rows.
    - The **Dashboard FAQ block** switch (off by default, on the Branding page) puts the FAQ on the portal dashboard.
    - A section named **Featured** (any capitalization) feeds the portal's featured help block. Renamed, the dashboard's FAQ block falls back to your first section and only the FAQ page's featured preview drops.
    - Saving replaces the whole FAQ; removed rows don't survive anywhere.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the questions your inbox answers weekly are in the FAQ.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

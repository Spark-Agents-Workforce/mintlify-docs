---
title: "When texts don't arrive"
description: "Texts have more ways to go quiet than emails. Five checks cover them all."
---

<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

Work the checks in order. If email is silent too, start at [When emails don't arrive](/platform-v2/run/when-emails-dont-arrive): that's the shared machinery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Twilio isn't connected, or isn't the route">
    - In **Settings > Integrations**, connect Twilio: the **Connect Twilio** modal takes your **Auth token**, **Account SID**, and a **Messaging Service** or **From number** (one of the two is required).
    - Under **Notification routing**, set **SMS notifications** to Twilio and **Save**; connected but not routed sends nothing, and the SMS settings page shows a banner.
    - Prove it: enter your own number under **Send a test SMS to (phone)** and click **Test connection**.
    - Full wiring: [Set up email and SMS](/platform-v2/run/set-up-email-and-sms).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the test lands on your phone.
    </Check>

    <Note>
    **Klaviyo can hold the SMS route instead.** Then Klaviyo flows decide what sends, logged under the Klaviyo channel; flows import as drafts that send nothing until set live (**Settings > Notifications > Email > View created flows & templates** lists each one's status). The phone only reaches Klaviyo for opted-in subscribers (login texts excepted), so check 3 still matters; the rest of this page assumes Twilio.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The text's event is switched off">
    - Open **Settings > Notifications > SMS texts**: every lifecycle text is a row with a switch and a preview; click a row to edit its message.
    - A new install starts with every lifecycle text off; only **Portal login code** and **Login link** read **Always on** and can't be switched.
    - Flip on the events you want, edit the copy (**Insert tag** for merge tags; the counter shows characters and SMS segments), and **Save**.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the event's switch is on.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/21-sms-texts.png" alt="Settings > Notifications > SMS texts: lifecycle texts with per-event switches, the login texts always on." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The subscriber never opted in, or has no phone">
    - Lifecycle texts go only to subscribers who opted in by texting START to your number; STOP opts them out.
    - Login codes and login links send regardless of consent, so a login text can arrive while lifecycle texts don't.
    - No phone on file means no text; the phone comes from Shopify, and subscribers can change it on their portal profile.

    <Note>
    **Consent is invisible in the admin.** There's no opt-in column, a blocked lifecycle text leaves no record, and the portal's SMS preference checkbox doesn't grant consent: only texting START does, so ask a silent subscriber to text START. It only counts from the phone number on their record; texting from another number stores nothing.
    </Note>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the subscriber has a phone on file and has texted START.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sent, but stuck at queued or never delivered">
    A text's status in the admin follows Twilio's receipts:

    | Status | What it means |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Queued** | Twilio accepted the text; the delivery receipt isn't back yet. |
    | **Sent** | Twilio confirmed delivery. |
    | **Failed** | Rejected, with Twilio's reason and numeric error code in the row. |
    | **Skipped** | The platform never handed it to Twilio: no SMS route, no phone, or no consent. Only login texts record this; a blocked lifecycle text leaves no row at all (check 3). |

    - Still **Queued** an hour later: check the message in Twilio's log. **Delivered** there means receipts aren't reaching your store (hand to whoever runs your code); **Sent** means the carrier never confirmed delivery, nothing to fix.
    - On **Settings > Operational health**, the **Integration connections** panel counts Twilio's failed deliveries over the last day and quotes the latest errors with codes; **View logs in Twilio** opens the full log.
    - In the Twilio Console, open **Monitor > Logs > Messaging** and read the message's status and error code: 21610, for example, means the recipient texted STOP.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** new texts move from queued to sent, and failures name their code.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replies do nothing">
    - SKIP, PAUSE, STOP and the rest need the **Incoming messages** URL set on your Twilio Messaging Service (or on the phone number, if you connected with just a number). The modal lists it, with the delivery-status URL, under **Webhooks: paste into your Twilio Messaging Service**; paste both.
    - The Messaging Service or From number in the modal must be the one whose webhooks point at your app; a mismatch drops every inbound text silently.
    - **Settings > Operational health** shows replies that arrived but failed as failed requests under **Integration connections**; no failed requests and no replies means the calls never arrive (the URL is missing or wrong).
    - Commands and their reply wording live under **Settings > Notifications > SMS replies**; what subscribers can do with them is on [When a subscriber asks for help](/platform-v2/run/when-a-subscriber-asks).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** text HELP to your Twilio number; any reply, even "don't recognize this number", proves the inbound wiring.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/22-twilio-modal.png" alt="The Manage Twilio modal: credentials, the two webhook URLs to paste into Twilio, and the test send." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Confirm the fix the same way as email.** The subscriber's page in admin lists every send under **Notifications** with channel and status; [When an alert opens](/platform-v2/run/when-an-alert-opens) covers the alert watching the sending pipeline.
</Note>

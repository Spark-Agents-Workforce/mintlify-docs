<span className="os-chip">2 min</span>

**Goal:** be signed in with a fresh run open, ready to enter your license key.

**You'll need:** the email inbox tied to your OpenSubs membership.

- Go to the [OpenSubs installer (Launchpad)](https://opensubs-launchpad-1076886952284.us-central1.run.app/) and click **Log in and start a new run**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/06-launchpad-home.png" width="620" alt="Launchpad home, signed out: Log in and start a new run." />
</Frame>

- Enter the email address associated with your membership, then enter the 6-digit code sent to that address.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/07-launchpad-login.png" alt="Login: membership email, then the 6-digit code." />
</Frame>

<Check>
**Checkpoint:** you're in, with a fresh run open. Keep your license key, the three Shopify values, and your dev store domain from [Gather the accounts](/platform-v2/setup/gather-the-accounts) at hand; the run asks for them next.
</Check>

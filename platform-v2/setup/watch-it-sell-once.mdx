<span className="os-chip">5 min</span>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy your own subscription">
    - Open your storefront and visit your product page. (Dev storefronts are password-gated; find the password in the dev store admin under **Online Store > Preferences**.) Under **Purchase options**, pick the subscription and add to cart.
    - Check out with Shopify's test gateway: card number **1**, any name, any future expiry, any CVC. (No test payment option at checkout? Activate the test gateway first: dev store admin, **Settings > Payments**.)

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/21-test-checkout.png" width="560" alt="Purchase options: the subscription selected, headed to test checkout." />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** order placed, no real money.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="See it in both places">
    - Find the subscription in the OpenSubs admin.
    - Still signed in as the test subscriber, open `/a/account` on the storefront; the account area loads with no login screen.

    <Columns cols={2}>
      <Frame>
        <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/22-admin-subscription.png" width="640" alt="The subscription in the OpenSubs admin." />
      </Frame>
      <Frame>
        <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/23-subscriber-portal.png" width="640" alt="/a/account: the subscriber portal, no login screen." />
      </Frame>
    </Columns>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** same subscription in both. The subscriber's receipt is Shopify's own order confirmation.
    </Check>

    <Note>
    **No welcome email is not a bug.** With no provider connected, the only receipt is Shopify's order confirmation, same as any order. Once SendGrid or Klaviyo is connected and the event is on, the **Subscription created** email takes that role (Klaviyo flows arrive as drafts).
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the health page">
    - In the OpenSubs admin, open **Settings > Operational health**.

    **You'll see:** five panels: alerts quiet, providers healthy, jobs green, webhooks delivering, queues quiet. The page the machine reports to from now on; reading it each morning is [The morning check](/platform-v2/run/the-morning-check).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/24-operational-health.png" width="560" alt="Operational health: jobs green, webhooks delivering, queues quiet." />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** green board. It sold once, and you own every piece of it.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Congratulations: the machine is yours

A shopper subscribed, Shopify wrote the contract, your engine mirrored it, the portal served it: every part lit, and green means proven. The installer left nothing behind but the machine itself: your database, your app, your store. Time spent: about an hour. Percentage owed on every order it ever takes: zero.

When you're ready for your real store, the next rung is [Live for New Orders](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/what-today-changes).

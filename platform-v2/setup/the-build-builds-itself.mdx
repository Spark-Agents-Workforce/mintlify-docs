<span className="os-chip">Build · ~5-10 min</span>

This step needs nothing from you. The header flips to **Provisioning your stack**, the installer takes over, and you can walk away.

While it runs, it builds and verifies your whole stack:

- Your database, with the scheduled jobs that keep the machine ticking (billing included).
- Your app, built and deployed to your Vercel account.
- Every webhook receiver (each one answers a signed test) and the channel your notifications ride on.

In the **Setup log**, each step gets a yellow checkmark as it passes, with a note on what was just verified.

<Note>
**You can't break the run.** Close the laptop or lose Wi-Fi and it picks up where it left off when you reopen. If a step fails, click **Retry**. Still stuck? Email [support@opensubs.com](mailto:support@opensubs.com) with your run ID (top of the installer).
</Note>

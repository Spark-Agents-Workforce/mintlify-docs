<span className="os-chip">Connect · ~10 min</span>

Connect is seven short prompts, all up front. Answer them and your part is mostly over. The header always says whose turn it is: **WAITING ON YOU** means you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The license key">
    - Enter your **license key** and click **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/08-license-key.png" alt="Connect: the license key prompt." />
    </Frame>

    - Review the running costs, a recurring floor of **Supabase Pro (\~\$25/mo) + Vercel Pro (\~\$20/mo)** plus usage, and click **I understand the costs**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/09-running-costs.png" alt="Connect: the running-costs acknowledgement." />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** license validated, costs acknowledged. 2 of 7.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Shopify values">
    The **Create your Shopify app** card asks for four values, and you gathered every one in [Gather the accounts](/platform-v2/setup/gather-the-accounts). It keeps them only long enough to provision, then purges them.

    - Paste the **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
    - Paste the **App Automation Token (deploy-only)**.
    - Enter your dev store's `.myshopify.com` domain from [Gather the accounts](/platform-v2/setup/gather-the-accounts) (your install link builds itself from it later), then click **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/10-shopify-app-card.png" width="620" alt="Connect: the Create your Shopify app card, four values." />
    </Frame>

    - On **Request Shopify scopes**, confirm the two requests you filed in [Gather the accounts](/platform-v2/setup/gather-the-accounts) (**Access subscriptions APIs** and **Read all orders**) show granted in the Dev Dashboard, then click **Access requested**. *"With Custom distribution every item below is approved instantly."* The data-use justification sits right there with a copy button if a form still wants it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/11-request-scopes.png" alt="Connect: Request Shopify scopes, approved instantly under Custom distribution." />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every item granted. No wait, no review queue.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The hosting connections">
    - Click **Connect Vercel**.
    - Click **Connect Supabase**.

    <Columns cols={2}>
      <Frame>
        <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/12-connect-vercel.png" alt="Connect: Connect Vercel." />
      </Frame>
      <Frame>
        <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/13-connect-supabase.png" alt="Connect: Connect Supabase." />
      </Frame>
    </Columns>

    - **Choose your region:** pick your Supabase organization and the region your subscriber data will live in, then click **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/14-supabase-region.png" alt="Connect: Supabase organization and region picker." />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** 7 of 7, nothing left to type. Build begins on its own.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

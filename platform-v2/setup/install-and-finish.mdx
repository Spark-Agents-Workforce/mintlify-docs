<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

<span className="os-chip">Go live · ~10 min</span>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install on the dev store">
    The run deploys your Shopify config itself (the app's URLs are written for you), then stops at **Install on your store**: the header is back to **WAITING ON YOU**.

    - Open your install link (already built from the domain you gave in [Connect your accounts](/platform-v2/setup/connect-your-accounts)) and install on your dev store. Shopify opens the app right after installing, and that first open connects your store automatically.
    - Come back to the run: it detects the connection, and **Verify scopes** passes by itself. (App never opened? Open it once from **Apps > OpenSubs**; it connects on open.)

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/16-install-connected.png" width="560" alt="Go live: installed and connected; Verify scopes passes on its own." />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** installed, connected, scopes verified. All without you.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure integrations, or skip">
    - In the dev store's admin, under **Apps > OpenSubs > Settings > Integrations**, add your SendGrid, Klaviyo, or Twilio keys (each lives in the provider's settings, under API keys) and send a test.
    - The same page also connects analytics, monitoring, and helpdesk tools; those can wait until [Set up analytics and monitoring](/platform-v2/run/set-up-analytics-and-monitoring) and [Set up Gorgias](/platform-v2/run/set-up-gorgias).
    - Under **Notification routing**, select the active email and SMS routes and save. SendGrid and Twilio send only when routed; Klaviyo receives events whenever connected, so its live flows send regardless of routing.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/16-integrations.png" alt="Settings > Integrations: the three providers connected, with the Notification routing card below." width="560" />
    </Frame>

    - Back in the run, click **Integrations configured**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/17-integrations.png" alt="Go live: the Integrations configured step." width="560" />
    </Frame>

    First connect provisions the provider for you: SendGrid gets every branded template created, Klaviyo a template and a draft flow per notification. (Klaviyo can carry SMS as well as email.)

    <Warning>
    Skipping is allowed, eyes open: with no provider configured, notifications are **discarded**, not queued for later. And if you use Klaviyo: imported flows start as **drafts**. Turn each flow live in Klaviyo or nothing sends, *including subscriber login codes*. [Set up email and SMS](/platform-v2/run/set-up-email-and-sms) shows each flow's status and the later **Update to latest** offer.
    </Warning>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** provider connected and tested, or deliberately skipped. Confirm.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The finish">
    The last step, **Verify the live stack**, checks the whole build end to end. Then the run completes and purges every secret it held. The screen says **Everything's installed.** Shoppers still see nothing: the storefront widgets stay off until you enable them.

    <Note>
    The run's one-time database password is purged with the rest: copy it before finishing, or reset it later in the Supabase dashboard.
    </Note>

    - Open the app from your Shopify admin (**Apps > OpenSubs**).
    - Go to **Settings > Extensions** and enable the **theme app embed**: it opens the theme editor with the embed ready; click **Save**. The widget can't render without it. Placing the widget itself is [Make something to sell](/platform-v2/setup/make-something-to-sell), on the same Extensions page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/dev-store-fast-path/18-everything-installed.png" width="640" alt="The completion screen: Everything's installed, secrets purged." />
    </Frame>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** run complete · secrets purged · theme app embed on · one widget left to place.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

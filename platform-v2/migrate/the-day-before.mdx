Five moves. The migration page prints the same playbook under **Before you start**, so the tool and this page tell one story.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Match your selling plans">
    - In **Settings > Selling plans**, mirror the old platform's plans: same frequencies, same discount levels.
    - Migrated subscriptions keep their exact price and cadence no matter what. But the options a subscriber can switch to come from your selling plans, so matching them means nothing looks different after the move.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every frequency you're migrating exists in your plans.
    </Check>

    {/* shot:16 ui-migration "Settings > Selling plans mirroring the old platform's frequencies and discounts." */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Freeze changes in the old app">
    - Announce a change freeze: no plan swaps, address edits, or pauses in the old app between export and import.
    - The export is a snapshot. Anything edited after it is silently lost.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the team knows the freeze window.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let payment retries finish">
    - In the old app, let in-flight failed-payment retries resolve, or cancel them.
    - Subscriptions caught mid-retry don't travel cleanly; OpenSubs imports a clean list.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** no subscriptions mid-retry.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide which discounts travel">
    - Nothing to do yet; just know the rule: permanent line discounts travel automatically. Discount codes and time-limited discounts don't.
    - The preview will name each one that can't travel. You either remove it in the old app or hold that subscriber back and migrate them in a later pass (partial migrations are normal).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** you know which discounts you're carrying.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export fresh">
    - On migration day, export subscriptions from the old app's dashboard. Skio and Stay AI: one file. Recharge: four (subscriptions, line items, addresses, payment methods). Loop and any other provider: map your export into the template files, with instructions on the upload screen. The screen shows the exact path for each provider.
    - Doing the optional [orders import](/platform-v2/migrate/bring-the-order-history)? Grab the orders export in the same sitting.
    - Fresh matters: every next charge date must still be in the future. A date that has slipped past blocks at the preview, and the fix is a fresh export, never a backdate.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** the row count roughly matches the old app's active-subscriber count.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

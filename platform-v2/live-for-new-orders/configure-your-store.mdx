Same moves you made on the dev store, sized to your real catalog. The Home checklist ticks itself off as you go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plans on real products">
    - In **Settings > Selling plans**, create your real plan groups and apply them to real products.
    - Plans live in *this* store; dev plans don't copy over.
    - Build the actual set of plans you want to sell: your "Monthly · 10% subscribe & save," plus the other frequencies and terms you intend to offer.
    - You'll see groups listed with product counts, and the Home checklist ticks **Create a selling plan**.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** real plans on real products.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/04-selling-plans.png" width="640" alt="Selling plans: real plan groups with product counts." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect email and SMS">
    - In **Settings > Integrations**, connect this store's email and SMS providers. Integrations are per-store: paste your keys again.
    - Under **Notification routing**, set the **Email notifications** and **SMS notifications** routes, then **Save**: one sender per event, or your subscriber gets two emails.
    - In **Settings > Notifications > Email**, click **Set up templates & flows** (Klaviyo) or **Set up email templates** (SendGrid). Branding bakes in at import; if you rebrand in the next step, hit **Re-import**.
    - Then do what the Integrations page warns: Klaviyo flows import as drafts, so turn them live in Klaviyo or login codes won't send. The same **Email** page shows each event's live-or-draft status.
    - In **Settings > Notifications**, turn on the events you need: a new install starts with every event off. On Twilio, also paste its two webhook URLs. [Set up email and SMS](/platform-v2/run/set-up-email-and-sms) is the full recipe.

    <Note>
    The same **Integrations** screen also connects analytics, helpdesk, and monitoring tools. Set them up now, or later via [Set up analytics and monitoring](/platform-v2/run/set-up-analytics-and-monitoring) and [Set up Gorgias](/platform-v2/run/set-up-gorgias).
    </Note>

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** providers connected; routing saved; flows live, not drafts.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/05-email-flows.png" width="640" alt="Notifications > Email: each event's templates and flows, with live-or-draft status." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand the portal">
    - In **Settings > Branding**, give the portal your real face: logo and colors start at defaults on a new store. [Brand the portal](/platform-v2/run/brand-the-portal) is the full recipe.
    - Add translations too, if you sell in more than one language.
    - Already imported email templates in the last step? Hit **Re-import** there so your emails wear the new look too.
    - Branding isn't alone: every OpenSubs setting on this store starts at defaults. Skim the Settings page and re-set anything you tuned on the dev store, like [payment recovery](/platform-v2/run/set-up-payment-recovery) or [rewards](/platform-v2/run/set-up-rewards).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** portal preview wears your brand.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/06-portal-branding.png" width="640" alt="Branding: the portal preview wearing your logo and colors." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a cancellation flow">
    - In **Settings > Cancellation flows**, build what a subscriber sees when they try to cancel: save offers first (a discount, a free gift, or a pause), cancel as the last resort. [Set up your cancellation flow](/platform-v2/run/set-up-your-cancellation-flow) is the full recipe.
    - The Home checklist ticks **Build a cancellation flow**, and your team test-drives the flow in [Prove it with real money](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/prove-it-with-real-money).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** a cancellation flow built, with at least one save offer.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/platform-v2/run/30-flow-builder.png" alt="The cancellation flow builder: the flow's reasons with their offers, stage by stage." width="560" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

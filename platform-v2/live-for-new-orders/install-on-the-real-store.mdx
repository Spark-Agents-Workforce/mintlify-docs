<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

<span className="os-chip">5 min</span>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the install link">
    - In the Dev Dashboard, open the app's **Distribution** page and copy the install link. Never generated one? Enter the live store's `.myshopify.com` domain and click **Generate link**.
    - You'll see an install link scoped to your app; this link *is* how a custom app reaches a real store.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** install link copied.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/02-install-link.png" width="560" alt="Distribution: the install link, generated and ready to copy." />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
    **On Shopify Plus?** The same screen offers "Allow multi-store installs for one Plus organization." Leave it unchecked for one production store; check it if sister stores in your organization will run OpenSubs too. Either way, dev stores stay fair game; the app's access carries wherever it installs.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install on your live store">
    - Open the link, confirm your live store, and approve the access screen (the same grants you approved when you created the app).
    - You'll see the OpenSubs admin open inside your real store's admin. Home greets you with a short checklist: **Migrate from another app** (optional) · **Create a selling plan** · **Connect email and SMS** · **Build a cancellation flow** · **Brand your customer portal**. That checklist is the next page, [Configure your store](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/configure-your-store); the optional Migrate item has [its own pages](/platform-v2/migrate/what-actually-moves), for when you're ready.
    - Meanwhile the app builds its mirror of your store: your real products, customers, and orders appear on their own. The sync plumbing set itself up at install; there is nothing for you to configure.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** OpenSubs admin open on the live store; Home checklist showing; catalog filling in.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/03-home-checklist.png" width="640" alt="The OpenSubs admin Home on the live store: the checklist, with the catalog filling in." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

title: "The swap"
description: "One theme save swaps the page."
---

import { PlatformTag } from "/snippets/PlatformTag.jsx";

<PlatformTag platform="opensubs" />

<span className="os-chip">10 min</span>

Your selling plans from [Configure your store](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/configure-your-store) are already on your products; this step makes the offer visible. The rule underneath: widgets decide what shoppers see, plans decide what bills.

<Note>
**Switching?** Leave the old platform's plan groups alone: attached, active, in their admin. Current subscriptions bill through them. Only their widget comes down, in the step below; that stops their new signups and nothing else.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place your widget">
    - Open the theme editor and go to your product template.
    - Add the **Subscription options** block near the buy buttons. The block hides itself on products with no plans, so placing it store-wide is safe.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/08-add-subscription-block.png" width="560" alt="Theme editor: adding the OpenSubs Subscription options block from the Apps tab." />
    </Frame>

    - If your theme needs the app embed for quick-view, enable it at **Settings > Extensions**.
    - Switching? Remove or hide the old platform's widget while you're in here.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/07-remove-old-widget.png" width="560" alt="Theme editor: the old platform's subscription widget coming off the product template." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check the page">
    - Click **Save**. The swap is done.
    - Your product page now offers one-time *and* your subscription plans, priced right.
    - Using a sidebar cart with a "switch to subscription" upgrade? Confirm it offers your OpenSubs plans now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
**Checkpoint:** exactly one subscription offer on the page: yours. If you're switching, the old platform's existing subscriptions are still billing, untouched.
</Check>

<Note>
Widget not showing later? [When the subscribe option disappears](/platform-v2/run/when-the-subscribe-option-disappears) is the fix; **Settings > Extensions** shows each surface's placement.
</Note>

<Note>
**The revert, if you ever need it.** Two steps, both on your side:

1. In the theme editor, take the **Subscription options** block back out.
2. In the OpenSubs admin, delete your plan groups at **Settings > Selling plans**.

Your offer is gone from the store. New store? You're selling one-time only, exactly as before. Switching? The old platform's plans and subscriptions never stopped; re-add its widget and its offer is back. Anything your team already sold on OpenSubs keeps billing until you cancel it. That's the whole undo.
</Note>

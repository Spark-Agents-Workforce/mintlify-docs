**New store?** Nothing to take down: skip the bullets marked **Switching?**

Swap in your offer:

- Your selling plans from [Configure your store](/platform-v2/live-for-new-orders/configure-your-store) are already on your products; this step makes the offer visible.
- Switching? Leave the old platform's plan groups alone: attached, active, in their admin. Current subscriptions bill through them. The swap happens in the theme: widgets decide what shoppers see, plans decide what bills.
- Open the theme editor and go to your product template. Add the **Subscription options** block near the buy buttons. Switching? Remove or hide the old platform's widget while you're in here; that stops their new signups and nothing else.
- Click **Save**. The swap is done.
- The block hides itself on products with no plans, so placing it store-wide is safe. If your theme needs the app embed for quick-view, enable it at **Settings > Extensions**.
- You'll see your product page offering one-time *and* your subscription spread, priced right.
- Using a sidebar cart with a "switch to subscription" upgrade? Confirm it now offers your plans, not the old platform's.

<Check>
**Checkpoint:** exactly one subscription offer on the page: yours. If you're switching, the old platform's existing subscriptions are still billing, untouched.
</Check>

<Note>
**The two-step revert, if you ever need it.** Both steps are on your side; the old platform is not involved.

1. In the theme editor, take the **Subscription options** block back out. Your widget vanishes from the page.
2. In the OpenSubs admin, delete your plan groups at **Settings > Selling plans**. Your subscription offer is gone from the store.

New store? Your products sell one-time only, exactly as before. Switching? The old platform's plans stayed attached and its subscriptions kept billing the whole time; re-add its widget and your page offers its subscriptions again. Contracts your team already sold on OpenSubs keep billing until you cancel them; nothing is left stranded. That's the whole undo.
</Note>

<Note>
**Nothing gets rebuilt today, and the installer does not run again.** Everything you set up carries over: your live store inherits the app, the database, and the scheduled jobs the moment it installs.
</Note>

- In your live store's Shopify admin, go to **Settings > Payments**.
- Confirm **Shopify Payments** is active, and that test mode is off.
- You'll see your real payout account and no orange test-mode banner.

<Check>
**Checkpoint:** Shopify Payments active on the live store, test mode off.
</Check>

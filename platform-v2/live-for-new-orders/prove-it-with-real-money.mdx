<Steps>
  <Step title="Your team buys">
    - No test cards this time: have one or several teammates subscribe on the live product page, with real cards and small stakes.
    - Spread the purchases on purpose: different plans, different frequencies, different terms.
    - You'll see an ordinary checkout, with the subscription terms on the line item.
    - Then find each order in three places: Shopify's order confirmation email, the contract in the OpenSubs admin, and the subscriber's portal: your store's domain followed by `/a/account`.
    - If your Klaviyo flow is live, or the SendGrid event is switched on, your branded welcome arrives too: your email setup's first real send.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** every order visible in all three places.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/09-subscriber-portal.png" width="640" alt="The subscriber's portal at /a/account: the team subscription, next order scheduled." />
    </Frame>

    <Columns cols={2}>
      <Frame>
        <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/10-admin-contract.png" width="640" alt="The contract in the OpenSubs admin: active, with its next billing date." />
      </Frame>
      <Frame>
        <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/11-order-confirmation.png" width="560" alt="Shopify's order confirmation: the subscription terms on the line item." />
      </Frame>
    </Columns>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The health check">
    - In the OpenSubs admin, open **Settings > Operational health**.
    - You'll see jobs green, webhooks delivering, queues quiet, and providers **Healthy** under **Integration connections**: the page the machine reports to from now on. Reading it daily is [The morning check](/platform-v2/run/the-morning-check).

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** Operational health green on the live store; providers **Healthy**.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/12-operational-health.png" width="560" alt="Operational health on the live store: jobs green, webhooks delivering, queues quiet." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clean up, or don't">
    - Refund the team orders in Shopify if you like, but remember: a refund is not a cancel. A subscription bills until its contract is cancelled.
    - Cancel the team's contracts in the portal. It's a free test-drive of your cancellation flow and its save offers: the discount, gift, or pause it tries before letting a subscriber go.
    - Or leave one running as a canary: a real contract the daily billing run charges on schedule, proof that renews itself every cycle. The billing run fires at 9:00 AM store time by default; change it in **Settings > Billing**.

    <Check>
    **Checkpoint:** team contracts cancelled, or one canary alive, on purpose.
    </Check>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/deploy/go-live-verification/13-cancel-success.png" width="440" alt="The portal's cancel path: a team contract cancelled." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
